A request for payment issued by a business to a customer, containing line items, taxes, discounts, and payment terms.

    <ResponseField name="id" type="string">

      Invoice ID.

      **Read-only.**

    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="revision" type="integer">

      Revision number, which increments by 1 each time the invoice is updated.
      To prevent conflicting changes,
      the current revision must be passed when updating the invoice.

      Ignored when creating an invoice.

      **Read-only.**

    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="createdDate" type="datetime">

      Date and time the invoice was created.

      **Read-only.**

    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="updatedDate" type="datetime">

      Date and time the invoice was last updated.

      **Read-only.**

    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="status" type="string">

      Invoice status.

      **Read-only.**

      **Supported values:**

      - `DRAFT` — Invoice is in draft status. Can be edited before publishing.
      - `PUBLISHING` — Invoice number is being allocated.
      - `PUBLISHED` — Invoice is published and ready for payment.
      - `PARTIALLY_PAID` — Invoice has received at least one payment but isn't fully paid.
      - `PAID` — Invoice is fully paid.
      - `VOIDED` — Invoice is voided and no longer valid.

    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="statusQualifiers" type="string[]">

      Additional flags providing context about the invoice's state, independent of its primary status. These flags are system-managed and read-only.

      **Read-only.** Constraints: `maxItems 2`.

      **Supported values:**

      - `SENT` — Invoice was sent to a customer.
      - `VIEWED` — Mark Viewed was called for the invoice.
      - `OVERDUE` — Invoice `dueDate` has passed and the invoice isn't fully paid or voided.

    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="availableActions" type="string[]">

      Actions that can be performed on this invoice based on its current status.

      **Read-only.** Constraints: `maxItems 11`.

      **Supported values:**

      - `DELETE` — Invoice can be deleted. Available when invoice is in `DRAFT` status.
      - `UPDATE` — Invoice can be updated. Available when invoice is in `DRAFT`, `PUBLISHED`, or `PARTIALLY_PAID` status.
      - `PUBLISH` — Invoice can be published. Available when invoice is in `DRAFT` status.
      - `PAY` — Payment can be recorded. Available when invoice is in `PUBLISHED` or `PARTIALLY_PAID` status.
      - `VOID` — Invoice can be voided. Available when invoice is in `PUBLISHED` or `PARTIALLY_PAID` status.
      - `SEND` — Invoice can be sent. Available when invoice is in `PUBLISHED` or later status.
      - `MARK_AS_VIEWED` — Invoice can be marked as viewed. Available when invoice isn't in `DRAFT` status.
      - `GENERATE_PDF_DOCUMENT` — PDF document can be generated. Not available while another PDF is being processed.
      - `ARCHIVE` — Invoice can be archived. Available when invoice isn't in `DRAFT` status and isn't already archived.
      - `UNARCHIVE` — Invoice can be unarchived. Available when invoice is archived.
      - `MARK_AS_SENT` — Invoice can be marked as sent. Available when invoice isn't in `DRAFT` status.

    </ResponseField>
