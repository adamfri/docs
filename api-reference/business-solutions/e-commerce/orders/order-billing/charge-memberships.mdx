    Sends a request to charge the memberships.
    The status will be updated asynchronously on the returned payments.

    ## Permissions

    This method requires one of the following permission scopes:

    <div className="wix-scopes">

    <span className="wix-scope" title="ECOM.ORDER_BILLING_CHARGE_MEMBERSHIPS">ECOM.ORDER_BILLING_CHARGE_MEMBERSHIPS</span>

    </div>

    Learn more about [app permissions](https://dev.wix.com/docs/build-apps/develop-your-app/access/authorization/about-permissions).

    ## Endpoint

    ```http
    POST https://www.wixapis.com/ecom/v1/order-billing/charge-memberships
    ```

    ## Response Object

    <ResponseField name="payments" type="Payment[]">

      Created payments for the charged memberships.

      Constraints: `minItems 1`, `maxItems 100`.

      <Expandable title="payments properties">

        <ResponseField name="id" type="string">

          Payment ID.

        </ResponseField>

        <ResponseField name="createdDate" type="datetime">

        </ResponseField>

        <ResponseField name="updatedDate" type="datetime">

          Date and time the payment was last updated in [ISO-8601](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISO_8601#Combined_date_and_time_representations) format.

          **Read-only.**

        </ResponseField>

        <ResponseField name="amount" type="Price">

          Payment amount.

          <Expandable title="amount properties">

            <ResponseField name="amount" type="string">

              Amount.

              Constraints: `>= 0`, `<= 1000000000000000`.

            </ResponseField>

            <ResponseField name="formattedAmount" type="string">

              Amount formatted with currency symbol.

              **Read-only.**

            </ResponseField>

          </Expandable>

        </ResponseField>

        <ResponseField name="refundDisabled" type="boolean">

          Whether refunds for this payment are disabled.
          + `true`: This payment is not refundable.
          + `false`: This payment may be refunded. However, this ultimately depends on the payment provider.

        </ResponseField>

        <ResponseField name="supportReceiptGeneration" type="boolean">

          Whether receipt generation is allowed for this payment.
          + `true`: This payment supports receipt generation.
          + `false`: This payment doesn't support receipt generation, or a receipt was already generated.

          **Read-only.** **Developer Preview.**

        </ResponseField>

        <ResponseField name="cashRounding" type="CashRoundingDetails">

          Details about cash rounding applied to this payment, when relevant.

          **Developer Preview.**

          <Expandable title="cashRounding properties">

            <ResponseField name="unroundedAmount" type="Price">

              Payment amount before cash rounding was applied.
              When provided, balance and status calculations use this value instead of `amount`
              to prevent false overpayment or underpayment due to rounding.

              **Developer Preview.**

              _Nested structure omitted for brevity — see the `Price` object._

            </ResponseField>

            <ResponseField name="roundingAdjustment" type="CashRounding">

              The difference between 'amount' and 'unroundedAmount' (amount minus unroundedAmount).
              A positive value indicates the price was rounded up; a negative value indicates a round-down.

              **Read-only.** **Developer Preview.**

              _Nested structure omitted for brevity — see the `CashRounding` object._

            </ResponseField>

          </Expandable>

        </ResponseField>

        <ResponseField name="status" type="string">

          Overall payment status.

          Gift card and membership payments support only `APPROVED`, `PENDING`, `CANCELED`, `DECLINED`, and `VOIDED`. The remaining values apply to regular payments.

          **Developer Preview.**

          **Supported values:**

          - `APPROVED` — Payment was charged successfully.
          - `PENDING` — Payment is being processed and hasn't been confirmed yet.
          - `PENDING_MERCHANT` — Payment is awaiting confirmation. For an offline payment, the merchant must confirm they received it. For an online payment, the payment provider hasn't confirmed it, which typically means the merchant's provider account setup isn't complete.
          - `CANCELED` — Payment was canceled before it was charged.
          - `DECLINED` — Payment was declined by the payment provider.
          - `REFUNDED` — Payment was fully refunded.
          - `PARTIALLY_REFUNDED` — Part of the payment amount was refunded.
          - `AUTHORIZED` — Funds are held on the buyer's payment method but haven't been captured. Capture them with [Capture Authorized Payments](https://dev.wix.com/docs/api-reference/business-solutions/e-commerce/orders/order-billing/capture-authorized-payments) or release them with [Void Authorized Payments](https://dev.wix.com/docs/api-reference/business-solutions/e-commerce/orders/order-billing/void-authorized-payments). An authorized payment can't be refunded until it's captured.
          - `VOIDED` — Payment was voided, releasing whatever it held or consumed: an authorization on the buyer's payment method, a gift card balance, or membership credits.

          Gift card and membership payments don't support partial refunds, so a full refund of one is reported as `VOIDED` rather than `REFUNDED`.

        </ResponseField>

        <ResponseField name="chargeCorrelationId" type="string">

          ID linking this payment to the charge request that created it. Matches the `idempotencyKey` sent in the Charge Order request.

        </ResponseField>

      </Expandable>

    </ResponseField>
