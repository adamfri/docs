---
title: "App Plans Object"
description: "The App Plans object in the Wix App Plans API."
icon: "cube"
---

<Tabs>

  <Tab title="REST">

    <ResponseField name="id" type="string">

      App ID, as defined in the [app dashboard](https://dev.wix.com/apps/my-apps?viewId=active-apps-view).

    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="plans" type="Plan[]">

      List of the app's pricing plans.

      Min: `0` plans
      Max: `50` plans

      Constraints: `minItems 0`, `maxItems 50`.

      <Expandable title="plans properties">

        <ResponseField name="id" type="string">

          ID of the app plan.

          **Read-only.** Constraints: `format GUID`.

        </ResponseField>

        <ResponseField name="vendorId" type="string">

          ID of your app's pricing plan, as displayed in the [app dashboard](https://dev.wix.com/apps/my-apps?viewId=active-apps-view).
          Identical to `vendorProductId` in the
          [Paid Plan Purchased webhook](https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/api-reference/app-management/apps/app-instance/paid-plan-purchased).

        </ResponseField>

        <ResponseField name="name" type="string">

          Name of your app's pricing plan, as defined by you in the [app dashboard](https://dev.wix.com/apps/my-apps?viewId=active-apps-view).

        </ResponseField>

        <ResponseField name="benefits" type="string[]">

          List of your plan's benefits, as defined by you in the [app dashboard](https://dev.wix.com/apps/my-apps?viewId=active-apps-view).
          Currently, the benefits are available only in English.

          Min: `0` benefits
          Max: `4` benefits
          Max per benefit: 1024 characters

          Constraints: `maxLength 1024`, `minItems 0`, `maxItems 4`.

        </ResponseField>

        <ResponseField name="prices" type="Price[]">

          List of the plan's prices. Available only when the plan's prices are managed
          by Wix and not externally.

          Min: `0` prices
          Max: `10` prices

          Constraints: `minItems 0`, `maxItems 10`.

          <Expandable title="prices properties">

            <ResponseField name="priceBeforeTax" type="string">

              Price without taxes. For yearly plans, Wix calculates and returns the
              average price per month. You can get the full price by multiplying the
              returned price by 12.

              Min: `0.00`
              Max: 1024 characters

              Constraints: `format DECIMAL_VALUE`, `maxLength 1024`, `>= 0.00`.

            </ResponseField>

            <ResponseField name="totalPrice" type="string">

              Total price including taxes.

              Min: `0.00`
              Max: 1024 characters

              Constraints: `format DECIMAL_VALUE`, `maxLength 1024`, `>= 0.00`.

            </ResponseField>

            <ResponseField name="billingCycle" type="Cycle">

              Information about the plan's recurring billing cycle or single payment.

              <Expandable title="billingCycle properties">

                <ResponseField name="cycleType" type="string">

                  Type of the billing cycle.

                  + `"UNKNOWN_UNIT"`:  There is no information about the billing cycle.
                  + `"ONE_TIME"`:  The customer pays for unlimited usage of the app with a single payment.
                  + `"RECURRING"`: The customer pays for a subscription to the app on a recurring schedule.

                  **Supported values:**

                  - `ONE_TIME`
                  - `RECURRING`

                </ResponseField>

                <ResponseField name="cycleDuration" type="Duration">

                  Duration of the billing cycle. Available only for `{"cycleType": "RECURRING"}`.

                  _Nested structure omitted for brevity — see the `Duration` object._

                </ResponseField>

              </Expandable>

            </ResponseField>

            <ResponseField name="usageBaseOptions" type="UsageBasedDetails">

              Details about the plan's usage-based pricing.
              Available only for plans with
              [usage-based pricing](https://dev.wix.com/docs/build-apps/build-your-app/pricing-plans/usage-based-pricing).

              <Expandable title="usageBaseOptions properties">

                <ResponseField name="minimumChargeIncrement" type="string">

                  Smallest possible amount that your app charges customers in usage-based
                  pricing. For example, the price of a single SMS message if your
                  app charges customers for sending text messages. Always in
                  [USD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_dollar).

                  Min: `0.00`
                  Max: 1024 characters

                </ResponseField>

                <ResponseField name="monthlyBaseFee" type="string">

                  Recurring, monthly base fee in usage-based pricing that your app
                  charges customers regardless of how much they use your app.

                  Min: `0.00`
                  Max: 1024 characters

                </ResponseField>

                <ResponseField name="customChargeDescription" type="string">

                  Description of the usage-based pricing plan, as defined in the [app dashboard](https://dev.wix.com/apps/my-apps?viewId=active-apps-view).

                  Max: 1024 characters

                </ResponseField>

              </Expandable>

            </ResponseField>

            <ResponseField name="discount" type="Discount">

              discount

              <Expandable title="discount properties">

                <ResponseField name="type" type="string">

                  **Supported values:**

                  - `FIXED_AMOUNT` — Discount is a fixed amount in USD.
                  - `PERCENTAGE` — Discount is a percentage of the total price.
                  - `DELAYED` — Billing is delayed rather than reduced - the price is unchanged but charged later (e.g. after a free trial).
                  The `amount` is `0` since nothing is discounted off the eventual price.

                </ResponseField>

                <ResponseField name="amount" type="string">

                  Discount amount

                  Constraints: `format DECIMAL_VALUE`, `maxLength 1024`, `>= 0.00`.

                </ResponseField>

                <ResponseField name="priceBeforeTax" type="string">

                  Price without taxes. For yearly plans, Wix calculates and returns the
                  average price per month. You can get the full price by multiplying the
                  returned price with 12.
                  Min: `0.00`
                  Max: 1024 characters

                  Constraints: `format DECIMAL_VALUE`, `maxLength 1024`, `>= 0.00`.

                </ResponseField>

                <ResponseField name="totalPrice" type="string">

                  Total price including taxes.
                  Min: `0.00`
                  Max: 1024 characters

                  Constraints: `format DECIMAL_VALUE`, `maxLength 1024`, `>= 0.00`.

                </ResponseField>

              </Expandable>

            </ResponseField>

            <ResponseField name="billingSource" type="string">

              **Supported values:**

              - `WIX` — plan billing and charges is managed by Wix
              - `EXTERNAL` — plan billing and charges is managed by the app

            </ResponseField>

            <ResponseField name="customPaymentTitle" type="string">

              Can be shown instead of plan price - used for dynamic plans

            </ResponseField>

          </Expandable>

        </ResponseField>

      </Expandable>

    </ResponseField>

  </Tab>
