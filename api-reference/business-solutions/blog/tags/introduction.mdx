The Tags API allows you to query and list tags to filter blog posts. Tags are a subset of categories for organizing blog posts, making it easier for site visitors to navigate and search for specific topics.

You can assign up to 30 tags per post, and each tag is limited to 50 characters.

With the Tags API, you can:

- [Create](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/blog/tags/create-tag) and manage tags to allow Wix users to categorize and organize their content.
- [Retrieve tags](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/blog/tags/get-tag) based on their [label](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/blog/tags/get-tag-by-label) or [slug](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/blog/tags/get-tag-by-slug) to allow site visitors to find and filter content.
- [Query tags](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/blog/tags/query-tags) to retrieve a list of tags based on specific criteria, providing site visitors with customized tag suggestions or recommendations.

## Terminology

- **Post**: A published article on a blog. It is also referred to as a publication. A blog can have up to 100,000 posts.
- **Draft Post**: An article written by a Wix user, blog editor, blog writer, or a guest writer that is not yet published.
- **Category**: A group of similar and related posts. A post can be assigned to a maximum of 10 categories.
- **Tag**: A way to further sort and organize blog posts. Tags provide the ability to filter blog posts.
