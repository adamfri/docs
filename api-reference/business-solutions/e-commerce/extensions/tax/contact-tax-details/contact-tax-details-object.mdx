---
title: "Contact Tax Details Object"
description: "Contact tax details hold the tax-related information for a single contact, keyed by the contact's ID."
icon: "cube"
---

Contact tax details hold the tax-related information for a single contact, keyed
by the contact's ID. Their main purpose is to associate a contact with a tax
exempt group, so that the contact receives the group's tax exemption
automatically at checkout.

Each contact's tax details map the contact to at most one tax exempt group.
During tax calculation, the buyer's contact ID resolves their tax details, and if
`taxExemptGroupId` is set, the matching exemption is applied and reported as an
exempt amount in the tax breakdown.

Contacts are typically added to a group by a site owner in the dashboard, or by a
VAT-validation app, such as Exemptify, that verifies a customer's VAT ID at
checkout and then associates the contact with the relevant exempt group. Once
associated, later checkouts by the same contact are exempt without re-entering the
VAT ID.

    <ResponseField name="id" type="string">

      Contact ID. Also serves as the ID of the contact tax details, since each contact has a single set of tax details.

    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="revision" type="integer">

      Revision number, which increments by 1 each time the contact tax details are updated.

      **Read-only.**

    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="createdDate" type="datetime">

      Date and time the contact tax details were created.

      **Read-only.**

    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="updatedDate" type="datetime">

      Date and time the contact tax details were updated.

      **Read-only.**

    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="taxExemptGroupId" type="string">

      ID of the tax exempt group the contact is associated with. Setting this value makes the contact eligible for the group's tax exemption at checkout.

    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="contactDetails" type="ContactDetails">

      The contact's details, such as name, email, and VAT ID.

      Returned only when `CONTACT_DETAILS` is passed to the `fields` array in the request.

      <Expandable title="contactDetails properties">

        <ResponseField name="firstName" type="string">

          Contact's first name.

          **Read-only.**

        </ResponseField>

        <ResponseField name="lastName" type="string">

          Contact's last name.

          **Read-only.**

        </ResponseField>

        <ResponseField name="company" type="string">

          Contact's company name.

          **Read-only.**

        </ResponseField>

        <ResponseField name="email" type="string">

          Contact's email address.

          **Read-only.**

        </ResponseField>

        <ResponseField name="vatId" type="string">

          Contact's VAT ID. Read from the contact's `invoices.vatId` extended field, so it's empty unless that field is set on the contact.

          **Read-only.**

        </ResponseField>

      </Expandable>

    </ResponseField>
