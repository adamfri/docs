    Sets whether a staff member is a service provider, meaning someone who provides bookable services to customers.

    Demoting a staff member to non-provider hides them from booking flows. Their
    working hours are preserved and existing bookings aren't affected.

    Promoting a staff member to service provider also grants the Wix user connected to them the
    Staff role, if they don't already have it. Demoting the staff member doesn't revoke that role.

    <Note>

      **Authentication**

      You can only call this method when authenticated as a Wix app or Wix user [identity](https://dev.wix.com/docs/api-reference/articles/authentication/about-identities).

    </Note>

    ## Permissions

    This method requires one of the following permission scopes:

    <div className="wix-scopes">

    <span className="wix-scope" title="SCOPE.DC-BOOKINGS.MANAGE-BOOKINGS">Manage Bookings</span>

    </div>

    Learn more about [app permissions](https://dev.wix.com/docs/build-apps/develop-your-app/access/authorization/about-permissions).

    Callable by: `APP`.

    ## Endpoint

    ```http
    POST https://www.wixapis.com/bookings/v1/staff-members/{staffMemberId}/set-service-provider
    ```

    ## Path Params

    <ParamField path="staffMemberId" type="string" required>

      ID of the staff member whose service provider status to set.

    </ParamField>

    ## Body Params

    <ParamField body="serviceProvider" type="boolean" required>

      Whether the staff member should be a service provider. When `true`, the staff
      member appears in booking flows, and the Wix user connected to them is granted
      the Staff role if they don't already have it.

      When `false`, the staff member no longer appears in booking flows. Their working
      hours are preserved, existing bookings aren't affected, and the Staff role isn't
      revoked.

    </ParamField>

    <ParamField body="fields" type="string[]">

      Conditional fields to return.

      Constraints: `maxItems 3`.

      **Supported values:**

      - `RESOURCE_DETAILS` — Includes `resource` in the response.
      - `ASSOCIATED_IDENTITY_STATUS` — Includes `associatedIdentityStatus` in the response.

    </ParamField>

    ## Response Object

    <ResponseField name="staffMember" type="StaffMember">

      Updated staff member.

      See the [Staff Member Object](/api-reference/business-solutions/bookings/staff-members/staff-members/staff-member-object) for this object's fields.

    </ResponseField>
