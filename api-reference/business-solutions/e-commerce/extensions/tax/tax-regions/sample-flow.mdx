This article presents a possible use case and sample flow that you can support. This can be a helpful jumping off point as you plan your implementation.

## Start charging tax in a new region

When a business starts expanding its operations it needs to start charging tax in that new region. 

To create a new tax region:

1. Call [Create Tax Region](https://dev.wix.com/docs/sdk/backend-modules/billing/tax-regions/create-tax-region) to create a new tax region to charge tax for.
2. Call [Tax Calculation Integration Service Plugin](https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-management/payments/service-plugins/tax-calculation-integration-service-plugin/introduction) to calculate tax, or, in [Tax Settings in the Wix dashboard](https://www.wix.com/my-account/site-selector/?buttonText=Select%20Site&title=Select%20a%20Site&autoSelectOnSingleSite=true&actionUrl=https:%2F%2Fwww.wix.com%2Fdashboard%2F%7B%7BmetaSiteId%7D%7D%2Fecom-platform/tax/), select the new region and set the tax rate to apply for all existing tax groups.
