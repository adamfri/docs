---
title: "Dispute History Record Object"
description: "A dispute history record represents a snapshot of dispute information at a specific point in time."
icon: "cube"
---

A dispute history record represents a snapshot of dispute information at a specific point in time.

Records are automatically created whenever a dispute changes status, stage, or other key properties, providing a complete audit trail for compliance and audit requirements.

<Tabs>

  <Tab title="REST">

    <ResponseField name="id" type="string">

      Dispute history record ID.

      **Read-only.** Constraints: `format GUID`, `immutable`.

    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="revision" type="integer">

      Revision number, which increments by 1 each time the dispute history record is updated.
      To prevent conflicting changes, the existing revision must be specified when updating a dispute history record.

      **Read-only.**

    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="createdDate" type="datetime">

      Date and time the dispute history record was created.

      **Read-only.**

    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="updatedDate" type="datetime">

      Date and time the dispute history record was last updated.

      **Read-only.**

    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="disputeId" type="string">

      ID of the dispute that this history record is a snapshot of.

      Constraints: `format GUID`, `immutable`.

    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="stage" type="string">

      Stage of the dispute process.

      **Read-only.** Constraints: `immutable`.

      **Supported values:**

      - `CHARGEBACK` — Formal dispute filed through the card network or issuing bank.
      - `INQUIRY` — Initial inquiry from the customer requesting information about the charge.

    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="status" type="string">

      Status of the dispute at the time this record was created.
      Indicates what action was required and by whom at this point in the dispute lifecycle.

      **Read-only.** Constraints: `immutable`.

      **Supported values:**

      - `WAITING_MERCHANT` — Waiting for merchant action, such as submitting evidence or accepting the dispute.
      - `UNDER_REVIEW` — Dispute is under review by the payment service provider (internal channel) or bank (external channel).
      - `WAITING_BUYER` — Waiting for buyer action or response.
      - `WON` — Dispute was resolved in favor of the merchant.
      - `LOST` — Dispute was resolved in favor of the cardholder.

    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="dueDate" type="datetime">

      The latest date and time until which the dispute can remain in its current status.

      If this date passes, the dispute will be resolved against the party that is required to take action. For example, if the status is `WAITING_MERCHANT`, and the due date passes, the dispute will be `LOST` and a refund will be processed automatically.

      **Read-only.**

    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="chargeId" type="string">

      ID of the charge that is being disputed.

      **Read-only.** Constraints: `format GUID`, `immutable`.

    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="channel" type="string">

      Channel through which the dispute is being processed.

      **Read-only.** Constraints: `immutable`.

      **Supported values:**

      - `INTERNAL` — Dispute is processed by the payment service provider.
      - `EXTERNAL` — Dispute is processed through the card network or issuing bank.

    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="extendedFields" type="ExtendedFields">

      Custom field data for the dispute history record object.

      [Extended fields](https://dev.wix.com/docs/build-apps/develop-your-app/extensions/backend-extensions/schema-plugins/about-schema-plugin-extensions) must be configured in the app dashboard before they can be accessed with API calls.

      <Expandable title="extendedFields properties">

        <ResponseField name="namespaces" type="object">

          Extended field data. Each key corresponds to the namespace of the app that created the extended fields.
          The value of each key is structured according to the schema defined when the extended fields were configured.

          You can only access fields for which you have the appropriate permissions.

          Learn more about [extended fields](https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/articles/getting-started/extended-fields).

          Constraints: `format map`.

          <Expandable title="namespaces properties">

            <ResponseField name="key" type="string" />

            <ResponseField name="value" type="object" />

          </Expandable>

        </ResponseField>

      </Expandable>

    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="tags" type="Tags">

      Tags assigned to the dispute history record for organization and filtering purposes.

      <Expandable title="tags properties">

        <ResponseField name="privateTags" type="TagList">

          Tags that require an additional permission in order to access them, normally not given to site members or visitors.

          <Expandable title="privateTags properties">

            <ResponseField name="tagIds" type="string[]">

              List of tag IDs.

              Constraints: `maxLength 5`, `maxItems 100`.

            </ResponseField>

          </Expandable>

        </ResponseField>

        <ResponseField name="publicTags" type="TagList">

          Tags that are exposed to anyone who has access to the labeled entity itself, including site members and visitors.

          <Expandable title="publicTags properties">

            <ResponseField name="tagIds" type="string[]">

              List of tag IDs.

              Constraints: `maxLength 5`, `maxItems 100`.

            </ResponseField>

          </Expandable>

        </ResponseField>

      </Expandable>

    </ResponseField>
