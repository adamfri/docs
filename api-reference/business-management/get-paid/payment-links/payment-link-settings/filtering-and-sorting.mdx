This article covers field support for filtering and sorting in [Query Payment Links](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-management/get-paid/payment-links/payment-links/query-payment-links).

|---|---|
|`createdDate`	|eq(),ne(),exists(),in(),hasSome(),lt(),le(),gt(),ge(),ascending(),descending()|
|`totalReceived.paymentCount`	|eq(),ne(),exists(),in(),hasSome(),lt(),le(),gt(),ge(),ascending(),descending()|
|`status`	|eq(),ne(),exists(),in(),hasSome(),ascending(),descending()|
|`paymentsLimit`	|eq(),ne(),exists(),in(),hasSome(),lt(),le(),gt(),ge(),ascending(),descending()|
|`source`	|eq(),ne(),exists(),in(),hasSome(),ascending(),descending()|
