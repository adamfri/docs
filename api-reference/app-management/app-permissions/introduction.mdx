The App Permissions API allows you to manage the permissions your app needs for its functionality. When users install your app, they need to grant your app the permissions it needs to allow it to access their site data and make API calls on their behalf. Permissions are granular and can be tailored to your app's specific needs, such as creating a [business location](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-management/locations/create-location) or [setting a member's privacy settings](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/crm/members-contacts/members/member-privacy-settings/set-member-privacy-settings).

Learn more [about permissions for Wix apps](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/build-apps/develop-your-app/access/authorization/about-permissions).

With the Wix App Permissions API, you can:

- [List permissions](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/app-management/app-permissions/list-app-permissions) associated with a specific app.
- [Create new permissions](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/app-management/app-permissions/create-app-permission) for an app.
- [Delete existing permissions](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/app-management/app-permissions/delete-app-permission) from an app.

<Note>

After adding or removing a permission, it might take up to 5 minutes for the update to appear across the [Wix ecosystem](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/build-apps/get-started/overview/the-wix-ecosystem).

</Note>

## Before you begin

It's important to note the following points before starting to code:

- You must have a [Wix app](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/build-apps/get-started/overview/about-wix-apps). Learn more [about developing apps](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/build-apps/develop-your-app/about-developing-apps).
- You must have an [API key](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/articles/get-started/api-keys) with the account-level **Manage Custom Apps** permission from the account that owns the app.
- [Identify the permissions your app needs](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/build-apps/develop-your-app/access/authorization/configure-permissions-for-your-app#step-1--identify-required-permissions) by checking which Wix APIs it uses.
