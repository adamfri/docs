This can be a helpful jumping off point as you plan your app's implementation.

## Manage an app embed's consent category

1. Call [List Apps And Storage](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/api-reference/business-management/cookie-consent-policy/consent-configs/list-apps-and-storage) to get apps embeds that are in use on a site.
1. Call [Create Consent Config](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/api-reference/business-management/cookie-consent-policy/consent-configs/create-consent-config) with the updated consent category to override the app embed's default category.
1. When relevant, call [Update Consent Config](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/api-reference/business-management/cookie-consent-policy/consent-configs/update-consent-config) to update the consent config.
