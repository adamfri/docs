    <div className="wix-ref">

    Retrieves analytics data, given the specified filtering.

    <Note>

      **Authentication**

      You can only call this method when authenticated as a Wix app or Wix user [identity](https://dev.wix.com/docs/api-reference/articles/authentication/about-identities).

    </Note>

    ## Permissions

    This method requires one of the following permission scopes:

    <div className="wix-scopes">

    <span className="wix-scope" title="SCOPE.DC-ANALYTICS-AND-REPORTS.READ-SITE-ANALYTICS">Site Analytics - read permissions</span>

    </div>

    Learn more about [app permissions](https://dev.wix.com/docs/build-apps/develop-your-app/access/authorization/about-permissions).

    Callable by: `APP`.

    ## Endpoint

    ```http
    GET https://www.wixapis.com/analytics-ng/v2/site-analytics/data
    ```

    ## Query Params

    <ParamField query="dateRange" type="DateRange" required>

      Date range.

      <Expandable title="dateRange properties">

        <ParamField query="startDate" type="string">

          Custom start date in [ISO 8601](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISO_8601) format.
          If the requested `startDate` is more than 61 days before the current date, a "Do not have data for this start date" error will be returned, as only 62 days of data is stored.

          Constraints: `format LOCAL_DATE`.

        </ParamField>

        <ParamField query="endDate" type="string">

          Custom end date in [ISO 8601](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISO_8601) format. Returned data will include all dates until the requested end date.
          For example, &#123; startDate: '2024-01-01', endDate: '2024-01-03' &#125; will return data for '2024-01-01' and '2024-01-02'.

          Constraints: `format LOCAL_DATE`.

        </ParamField>

      </Expandable>

    </ParamField>

    <ParamField query="timeZone" type="string">

      Time zone.

    </ParamField>

    <ParamField query="measurementTypes" type="string[]" required>

      Measurement types.

      Constraints: `maxItems 100`.

      **Supported values:**

      - `TOTAL_SALES` — Total amount of money expected from all product and service sales, before deducting refunds, shipping, and fees, including both online and manual payments.
      - `TOTAL_ORDERS` — Total amount of income from sales after deducting refunds.
      - `CLICKS_TO_CONTACT` — Total amount of site sessions where a visitor clicked to contact you via WhatsApp, phone or email.
      - `TOTAL_SESSIONS` — Total amount of visitor sessions.
      - `TOTAL_FORMS_SUBMITTED` — Total amount of forms submitted.
      - `TOTAL_UNIQUE_VISITORS` — Total amount of unique visitors.

    </ParamField>

    ## Response Object

    <ResponseField name="data" type="MeasureItem[]">

      Analytics data per type.

      <Expandable title="data properties">

        <ResponseField name="type" type="string">

          Measurement type.

          **Supported values:**

          - `TOTAL_SALES` — Total amount of money expected from all product and service sales, before deducting refunds, shipping, and fees, including both online and manual payments.
          - `TOTAL_ORDERS` — Total amount of income from sales after deducting refunds.
          - `CLICKS_TO_CONTACT` — Total amount of site sessions where a visitor clicked to contact you via WhatsApp, phone or email.
          - `TOTAL_SESSIONS` — Total amount of visitor sessions.
          - `TOTAL_FORMS_SUBMITTED` — Total amount of forms submitted.
          - `TOTAL_UNIQUE_VISITORS` — Total amount of unique visitors.

        </ResponseField>

        <ResponseField name="values" type="MeasureValue[]">

          Data values per date.

          Constraints: `maxItems 100`.

          <Expandable title="values properties">

            <ResponseField name="date" type="string">

              Date in [ISO 8601](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISO_8601) format.

              Constraints: `format LOCAL_DATE`.

            </ResponseField>

            <ResponseField name="value" type="number">

              Value.

            </ResponseField>

          </Expandable>

        </ResponseField>

        <ResponseField name="total" type="number">

          Total value.

        </ResponseField>

      </Expandable>

    </ResponseField>
