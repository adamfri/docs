    Charges payments for an order using one or more payment methods.

    Supports charging through multiple payment methods in a single request:
    - **Regular payments**: Charges using the customer's payment method (credit card, PayPal, etc.).
    - **Gift cards**: Redeems gift card codes
    - **Memberships**: Charges membership credits or benefits for specific line items.

    The method processes all specified payment types and returns the created payments
    along with the updated order transactions.

    A successful charge returns payments with a `status` of `APPROVED`, `AUTHORIZED`, `PENDING`, or `PENDING_MERCHANT`.
    `PENDING` and `PENDING_MERCHANT` payments are confirmed asynchronously, so poll the order's transactions
    or listen for payment events rather than treating the response as final.

        <ResponseField name="status" type="string">

          Overall payment status.

          Gift card and membership payments support only `APPROVED`, `PENDING`, `CANCELED`, `DECLINED`, and `VOIDED`. The remaining values apply to regular payments.

          **Developer Preview.**

          **Supported values:**

          - `APPROVED` — Payment was charged successfully.
          - `PENDING` — Payment is being processed and hasn't been confirmed yet.
          - `PENDING_MERCHANT` — Payment is awaiting confirmation. For an offline payment, the merchant must confirm they received it. For an online payment, the payment provider hasn't confirmed it, which typically means the merchant's provider account setup isn't complete.
          - `CANCELED` — Payment was canceled before it was charged.
          - `DECLINED` — Payment was declined by the payment provider.
          - `REFUNDED` — Payment was fully refunded.
          - `PARTIALLY_REFUNDED` — Part of the payment amount was refunded.
          - `AUTHORIZED` — Funds are held on the buyer's payment method but haven't been captured. Capture them with [Capture Authorized Payments](https://dev.wix.com/docs/api-reference/business-solutions/e-commerce/orders/order-billing/capture-authorized-payments) or release them with [Void Authorized Payments](https://dev.wix.com/docs/api-reference/business-solutions/e-commerce/orders/order-billing/void-authorized-payments). An authorized payment can't be refunded until it's captured.
          - `VOIDED` — Payment was voided, releasing whatever it held or consumed: an authorization on the buyer's payment method, a gift card balance, or membership credits.

          Gift card and membership payments don't support partial refunds, so a full refund of one is reported as `VOIDED` rather than `REFUNDED`.

    | `428` | `FAILED_PRECONDITION` | `ORDER_CANCELED` | Order was already canceled. |
    | `428` | `FAILED_PRECONDITION` | `ORDER_PAID` | Order was already paid. |

    | `428` | `FAILED_PRECONDITION` | `GIFT_CARD_EXPIRED` | Gift card is expired. |
    | `428` | `FAILED_PRECONDITION` | `GIFT_CARD_DISABLED` | Gift card is disabled. |
    | `428` | `FAILED_PRECONDITION` | `INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS` | Gift card has insufficient funds. |
    | `428` | `FAILED_PRECONDITION` | `INVALID_GIFT_CARD_CURRENCY` | Gift card currency is different from order currency. |
    | `409` | `ALREADY_EXISTS` | `GIFT_CARD_ALREADY_APPLIED` | Gift card was already applied to the order. |
    | `409` | `ALREADY_EXISTS` | `ALREADY_REDEEMED` | Gift card was already redeemed. |
    | `428` | `FAILED_PRECONDITION` | `MEMBERSHIP_NOT_ELIGIBLE` | Membership eligibility check failed. |
    | `409` | `ALREADY_EXISTS` | `LINE_ITEM_PAID` | Requested line item is already paid with membership. |
    | `428` | `FAILED_PRECONDITION` | `PAYMENT_ATTEMPT_EXPIRED` | The payment attempt expired before it could be charged. Collect the payment details again and retry the charge. |
    | `428` | `FAILED_PRECONDITION` | `PAYMENT_METHOD_UNAVAILABLE` | Payment method is unavailable. It's either disabled or not connected to the site. |
    | `428` | `FAILED_PRECONDITION` | `PAYMENT_ALREADY_APPROVED` | The payment was already approved. Unlike `ORDER_PAID`, the order isn't necessarily fully paid. Retrieve the order's payments instead of charging again. |

    <Info>

      **Developer Preview.** This method is in Developer Preview and may change without notice.

    </Info>

    Charges payments for an order using one or more payment methods.

    Supports charging through multiple payment methods in a single request:
    - **Regular payments**: Charges using the customer's payment method (credit card, PayPal, etc.).
    - **Gift cards**: Redeems gift card codes
    - **Memberships**: Charges membership credits or benefits for specific line items.

    The method processes all specified payment types and returns the created payments
    along with the updated order transactions.

    A successful charge returns payments with a `status` of `APPROVED`, `AUTHORIZED`, `PENDING`, or `PENDING_MERCHANT`.
    `PENDING` and `PENDING_MERCHANT` payments are confirmed asynchronously, so poll the order's transactions
    or listen for payment events rather than treating the response as final.
