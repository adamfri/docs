<Note>This article applies to the REST API only.</Note>

The eCommerce Order Transactions API concentrates records and details of payments and refunds associated with eCommerce orders.

With the Order Transactions API you can:

* Retrieve transactions related to a [single](https://dev.wix.com/docs/api-reference/business-solutions/e-commerce/orders/order-transactions/list-transactions-for-single-order) or [multiple](https://dev.wix.com/docs/api-reference/business-solutions/e-commerce/orders/order-transactions/list-transactions-for-multiple-orders) orders.
* [Add payment records](https://dev.wix.com/docs/api-reference/business-solutions/e-commerce/orders/order-transactions/add-payments) to an order.
* Update the status of a [single payment](https://dev.wix.com/docs/api-reference/business-solutions/e-commerce/orders/order-transactions/update-payment-status) or [multiple payments](https://dev.wix.com/docs/api-reference/business-solutions/e-commerce/orders/order-transactions/bulk-update-payment-statuses).
* Listen to events when an order's [transactions are updated](https://dev.wix.com/docs/api-reference/business-solutions/e-commerce/orders/order-transactions/order-transactions-details-updated).

## Before you begin

+ The Order Transactions API records payments and refunds. It doesn't charge or refund funds. Adding a payment record or updating a payment status has no effect on the actual money movement.
+ To issue a refund, use [Refund Payments](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/api-reference/business-solutions/e-commerce/orders/order-billing/refund-payments) in the Order Billing API. The refund is then recorded in the order's transactions.
+ After a payment record is added or updated, the order's `paymentStatus` is recalculated asynchronously. Reading the order immediately afterward may still show the previous payment status.

## Terminology

* **Transaction**: Global term for a transfer of funds. This can be either one of:
  * **Payment**: A transfer of funds from customer to merchant/seller when making a purchase.
  * **Refund**: A reimbursing transfer of funds back to the buyer.
* **Chargeback**: A payment reversal initiated by the buyer's bank or card issuer, recorded on the relevant payment.
