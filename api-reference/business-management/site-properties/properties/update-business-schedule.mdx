    Updates a site's business schedule.

    <Note>

    This method overwrites the current business schedule. `businessSchedule` is an array, so it must be written in its entirety. Therefore, you must modify the existing array and specify it in the API call.

    </Note>

    <Note>

      **Authentication**

      You can only call this method when authenticated as a Wix app or Wix user [identity](https://dev.wix.com/docs/api-reference/articles/authentication/about-identities).

    </Note>

    ## Permissions

    This method requires one of the following permission scopes:

    <div className="wix-scopes">

    <span className="wix-scope" title="SCOPE.DC-SITEPROP.MANAGE-BUSINESS-SCHEDULE">Manage Business Schedule</span>

    </div>

    Learn more about [app permissions](https://dev.wix.com/docs/build-apps/develop-your-app/access/authorization/about-permissions).

    Callable by: `APP`.

    ## Endpoint

    ```http
    POST https://www.wixapis.com/site-properties/v4/properties/business-schedule
    ```

    ## Body Params

    <ParamField body="businessSchedule" type="BusinessSchedule">

      The site's business schedule in the site time zone.

      <Expandable title="businessSchedule properties">

        <ParamField body="periods" type="TimePeriod[]">

          Weekly recurring time periods when the business is regularly open or the service is available. Limited to 100 time periods.

          Constraints: `maxItems 100`.

          <Expandable title="periods properties">

            <ParamField body="openDay" type="string">

              Day of the week the period starts on.

              **Supported values:**

              - `MONDAY`
              - `TUESDAY`
              - `WEDNESDAY`
              - `THURSDAY`
              - `FRIDAY`
              - `SATURDAY`
              - `SUNDAY`

            </ParamField>

            <ParamField body="openTime" type="string">

              Time the period starts in 24-hour [ISO 8601](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISO_8601) extended format. Valid values are `00:00` to `24:00`, where `24:00` represents
              midnight at the end of the specified day.

            </ParamField>

            <ParamField body="closeDay" type="string">

              Day of the week the period ends on.

              **Supported values:**

              - `MONDAY`
              - `TUESDAY`
              - `WEDNESDAY`
              - `THURSDAY`
              - `FRIDAY`
              - `SATURDAY`
              - `SUNDAY`

            </ParamField>

            <ParamField body="closeTime" type="string">

              Time the period ends in 24-hour [ISO 8601](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISO_8601) extended format. Valid values are `00:00` to `24:00`, where `24:00` represents
              midnight at the end of the specified day.

              __Note:__ If `openDay` and `closeDay` specify the same day of the week `closeTime` must be later than `openTime`.

            </ParamField>

          </Expandable>

        </ParamField>

        <ParamField body="specialHourPeriod" type="SpecialHourPeriod[]">

          Exceptions to the business's regular hours. The business can be open or closed during the exception.

          Constraints: `maxItems 100`.

          <Expandable title="specialHourPeriod properties">

            <ParamField body="startDate" type="string">

              Start date and time of the exception in [ISO 8601](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISO_8601) format and [Coordinated Universal Time (UTC)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coordinated_Universal_Time).

            </ParamField>

            <ParamField body="endDate" type="string">

              End date and time of the exception in [ISO 8601](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISO_8601) format and [Coordinated Universal Time (UTC)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coordinated_Universal_Time).

            </ParamField>

            <ParamField body="isClosed" type="boolean">

              Whether the business is closed (or the service is not available) during the exception.

              Default: `true`.

            </ParamField>

            <ParamField body="comment" type="string">

              Additional info about the exception. For example, "We close earlier on New Year's Eve."

            </ParamField>

          </Expandable>

        </ParamField>

      </Expandable>

    </ParamField>
