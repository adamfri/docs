The Tips API allows you to manage tips for
[eCommerce orders](https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/e-commerce/orders/introduction).
You can also preview tips and how they would be distributed to staff members
before an order is created.

With the Tips API, you can:
+ Preview tips.
+ Apply or remove tips from eCommerce orders.
+ Retrieve tips and their distributions.

See also:
+ [Tip Settings API](https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/e-commerce/tips/tip-settings/introduction)
+ [Tippable Staff API](https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/e-commerce/tips/tippable-staff/introduction)

## Before you begin

It's important to note the following points before starting to code:

+ Ensure that a site has installed [Wix Tips](fallback::https://www.wix.com/app-market/wix-tips). 
  Wix Tips is automatically installed with every
  [Wix Bookings](fallback::https://www.wix.com/app-market/bookings) installation.
+ Each tip is applied to an [eCommerce order](https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/e-commerce/orders/order-object)
  as a single `additionalFee`. The tip's ID is stored in the `additionalFee.code` field of the order.
+ You can charge customers in separate transactions for individual tip
  distributions. After a tip distribution has an associated transaction,
  its amount can't be modified in the future.
+ Currently, only [Bookings staff members](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/bookings/staff-members/introduction)
  and [site collaborators](fallback::https://support.wix.com/en/article/inviting-people-to-contribute-to-your-site)
  can receive tip distributions.

## Terminology

+ **Tip**: Details the tip amount, how it’s distributed among staff, and the
  associated order’s line item subtotal.
+ **Tip distribution**: How the total tip is divided among staff. The business
  receives the undistributed tip amount.
+ **Tip settings**: Configures how tips are previewed, applied, and distributed.
  Refer to the [Tip Settings API](https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/e-commerce/tips/tip-settings/introduction)
  for more information.
+ **Staff**: Team members who receive a portion of the tip. Currently, only
  [Bookings staff members](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/bookings/staff-members/introduction)
  and [site collaborators](fallback::https://support.wix.com/en/article/inviting-people-to-contribute-to-your-site)
  can receive tip distributions. Refer to the
  [Tippable Staff API](https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/e-commerce/tips/tippable-staff/introduction)
  for more information.
+ **eCommerce order**: Contains information about purchased items, price and tax
  summaries, shipping, and billing details, any applied discounts, and the status
  of payment and fulfillment. Refer to the
  [eCommerce Orders API](https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/e-commerce/orders/introduction)
  for more information.

For a comprehensive glossary of Wix Bookings terms, see
