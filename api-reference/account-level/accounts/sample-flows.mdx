This article shares some typical use cases you can support, as well as an example flow that supports each use case. 
You're certainly not limited to these use cases, but they can be a helpful jumping off point as you plan your implementation.

## Create a Wix account for a customer 
When creating a Wix account for a customer, you can choose whether to add the customer as a collaborator immediately or not.

- **If your customer should become a collaborator (also known as a contributor) on the site immediately upon account creation:** 
Call [Create Account as Collaborator](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/api-reference/account-level/user-management/accounts/accounts/create-account-as-collaborator), with the relevant email and SSO identities, the roles to assign (available by calling [Get Roles Info](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/api-reference/account-level/user-management/accounts/users/get-roles-info)) and the ID of the site where they will be added as a collaborator.

- **If your customer shouldn't become a site collaborator at this point:**
Call [Create Account](https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/account-level/accounts/create-account), with the relevant email and SSO identities. You can add the customer as a collaborator to the site at a later date by calling [Change Role](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/api-reference/account-level/user-management/contributors/change-role).  

The account that is created for the customer is a child account under your company's parent account, sometimes referred to as a managed account.  
The customer will be provided their own Wix account ID and user ID. Note that a new user ID will be created even if the customer already has their own Wix account with the same email address.  
The customer's site will inherit any [custom branding](fallback::https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-studio-setting-up-custom-branding) from your company's account, as well as some configuration, based on your company's contract with Wix.
   
## Retrieve child accounts created under your company's account
If you create accounts for your customers, you can retrieve a list of all account IDs created under your account by calling [List Child Accounts](https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/account-level/accounts/list-child-accounts).   
If you maintain management access to your customers' accounts, Wix recommends mapping these account IDs to your customers, so you can easily make account-level API calls that require passing an account ID in the header.
