    Adds a media item to a location.

    Specify `sourceUrl`, a publicly reachable URL that Google fetches the image
    from. The URL has to stay reachable until Google has fetched it. Returns the
    created item, including the media key needed to delete it.

    Two things to plan for:

    - Google doesn't deduplicate by URL. Calling this twice with the same
    `sourceUrl` creates two media items, so track what you've already added.
    - Google allows about 10 edits per minute per Business Profile, and that budget
    covers every write to the profile: adding and deleting media, but also Update
    Google Location and Update Location Attributes. Interleaving those with media
    writes brings the 429 closer, so pace the profile as a whole rather than each
    method separately. Treat a 429 as worth retrying after a delay rather than as
    a rejection of the image.

    This method doesn't pace itself, so use it for single changes. To add several
    photos at once, call Bulk Create Media Uploads instead. That queues the whole
    set and applies it to Google gradually, which is what the per-minute budget
    makes necessary.

    Errors:
    - `NOT_FOUND`: the location isn't recorded for this site. Google isn't called.
    - `GOOGLE_API_CALL_FAILED`: the call to Google failed. Google's status and
    response are in the error payload.

    <Note>

      **Authentication**

      You can only call this method when authenticated as a Wix app or Wix user [identity](https://dev.wix.com/docs/api-reference/articles/authentication/about-identities).

    </Note>

    ## Endpoint

    ```http
    POST https://www.wixapis.com/gbp/v1/locations/{locationId}/media
    ```

    ## Path Params

    <ParamField path="locationId" type="string" required>

      Location ID. This is Google's location identifier.

    </ParamField>

    ## Body Params

    <ParamField body="sourceUrl" type="string" required>

      Publicly accessible URL of the media file to add. Google fetches the bytes
      from this URL server-side, so it must be reachable without auth.

      Constraints: `format WEB_URL`, `maxLength 2048`.

    </ParamField>

    <ParamField body="category" type="string" required>

      Where the media is shown on the listing (e.g. EXTERIOR, INTERIOR, LOGO).

      **Supported values:**

      - `COVER` — The banner image at the top of the profile. One per location.
      - `PROFILE` — The profile image shown alongside the business name.
      - `LOGO` — The business's logo. One per location.
      - `EXTERIOR` — The outside of the premises, including the storefront.
      - `INTERIOR` — The inside of the premises.
      - `PRODUCT` — A product the business sells.
      - `AT_WORK` — The team at work, such as staff serving customers.
      - `FOOD_AND_DRINK` — Food or drink the business serves.
      - `MENU` — The menu, for a business that serves food or drink.
      - `COMMON_AREA` — A shared space, such as a lobby or a waiting area.
      - `ROOMS` — Guest rooms, for accommodation businesses.
      - `TEAMS` — The people who work at the business.
      - `ADDITIONAL` — Anything that doesn't fit the other categories. This is the default for a
      gallery photo.

    </ParamField>

    <ParamField body="format" type="string" required>

      PHOTO or VIDEO.

      **Supported values:**

      - `PHOTO`
      - `VIDEO`

    </ParamField>

      The created media item.

        <ResponseField name="mediaKey" type="string">

          Google-hosted URL the media is served at.

          than the stream.

          Constraints: `format WEB_URL`, `maxLength 2048`.

        </ResponseField>

        <ResponseField name="category" type="string">

          Category of the media (e.g. EXTERIOR, INTERIOR, LOGO).

          **Supported values:**

          - `COVER` — The banner image at the top of the profile. One per location.
          - `PROFILE` — The profile image shown alongside the business name.
          - `LOGO` — The business's logo. One per location.
          - `EXTERIOR` — The outside of the premises, including the storefront.
          - `INTERIOR` — The inside of the premises.
          - `PRODUCT` — A product the business sells.
          - `AT_WORK` — The team at work, such as staff serving customers.
          - `FOOD_AND_DRINK` — Food or drink the business serves.
          - `MENU` — The menu, for a business that serves food or drink.
          - `COMMON_AREA` — A shared space, such as a lobby or a waiting area.
          - `ROOMS` — Guest rooms, for accommodation businesses.
          - `TEAMS` — The people who work at the business.
          - `ADDITIONAL` — Anything that doesn't fit the other categories. This is the default for a
          gallery photo.

        </ResponseField>

        <ResponseField name="thumbnailUrl" type="string">

          Google-hosted thumbnail URL. Like `googleUrl`, this can change over time.

          Constraints: `format WEB_URL`, `maxLength 2048`.

        </ResponseField>

        <ResponseField name="description" type="string">

          Caption or description of the media item.

          Constraints: `maxLength 5000`.

        </ResponseField>

        <ResponseField name="sourceUrl" type="string">

          The URL the image was originally fetched from, echoing the `sourceUrl` it
          was created with. Present on media the site owner posted, and absent on
          media contributed by customers.

          Constraints: `format WEB_URL`, `maxLength 2048`.

        </ResponseField>

        <ResponseField name="attribution" type="MediaItemAttribution">

          Attribution of the contributor, for customer-contributed media. Empty for
          merchant-posted media (e.g. items added via CreateMedia).

          <Expandable title="attribution properties">

            <ResponseField name="profileName" type="string">

              Display name of the contributor.

              Constraints: `maxLength 255`.

            </ResponseField>

            <ResponseField name="profilePhotoUrl" type="string">

              URL of the contributor's profile photo.

              Constraints: `format WEB_URL`, `maxLength 2048`.

            </ResponseField>

            <ResponseField name="takedownUrl" type="string">

              URL to report the media or request its takedown.

              Constraints: `format WEB_URL`, `maxLength 2048`.

            </ResponseField>

            <ResponseField name="profileUrl" type="string">

              URL of the contributor's profile.

              Constraints: `format WEB_URL`, `maxLength 2048`.

            </ResponseField>

          </Expandable>

        </ResponseField>

      </Expandable>

    </ResponseField>
