## Add a new sender to your email campaign

This use case demonstrates how to add a new sender to an email campaign. In this example, a business now accepts bookings and wants to start a new email campaign, where the new sender's email address is `bookings@website.com`.

<Note>

This flow assumes a site already has the Core or Advanced Email Marketing plans.

</Note>

1. Call [Create Sender Email](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-management/marketing/sender-emails/create-sender-email) and specify the email address to set as a sender’s email address.
1. Call [Send Verification Code](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-management/marketing/sender-emails/send-verification-code) and check the inbox of that email address for the verification code.
1. Use the received verification code to call [Verify Sender Email](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-management/marketing/sender-emails/verify-sender-email) and verify the email address.
1. Call [Create Sender Details](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-management/marketing/sender-details/create-sender-details) passing the verified email address into the `fromEmailAddress` field. Also, enter the sender's name into the `fromName` field.
