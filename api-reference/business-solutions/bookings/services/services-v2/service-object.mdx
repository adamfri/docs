    <ResponseField name="payment" type="Payment">

      Payment options for booking the service.
      Learn more about [service payments](https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/bookings/services/services-v2/about-service-payments).

      <Expandable title="payment properties">

        <ResponseField name="rateType" type="string">

          The rate the customer is expected to pay for the service.

          **Supported values:**

          - `FIXED` — The service has a fixed price.
          - `CUSTOM` — The service has a custom price, expressed as a price description.
          - `VARIED` — This service is offered with a set of different prices based on different terms.
          - `NO_FEE` — This service is offered free of charge.
          - `SUBSCRIPTION` — The service charges recurring monthly payments. Supported for course services only.

        </ResponseField>

        <ResponseField name="options" type="PaymentOptions">

          The payment options a customer can use to pay for the service.

          <Expandable title="options properties">

            <ResponseField name="online" type="boolean">

              Customers can pay for the service online.
              When `true`:
              + `rateType` must be `FIXED`, `VARIED`, or `SUBSCRIPTION`.
              + `fixed.price`, `varied.defaultPrice`, or `subscription.amountPerBillingCycle` must be specified respectively.

              Required when: `rateType` is `SUBSCRIPTION`.
              Read more about [getting paid online](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-bookings-about-getting-paid-online).

            </ResponseField>

            <ResponseField name="inPerson" type="boolean">

              Customers can pay for the service in person.

            </ResponseField>

            <ResponseField name="deposit" type="boolean">

              This service requires a deposit to be made online in order to book it.
              When `true`:
              + `rateType` must be `VARIED` or `FIXED`.
              + A `deposit` must be specified.
              + `online` must be `true`.
              + `inPerson` must be `false`.

            </ResponseField>

            <ResponseField name="pricingPlan" type="boolean">

              Whether customers can pay for the service using a pricing plan.
              Read more about [service payment options](https://dev.wix.com/docs/api-reference/business-solutions/bookings/services/services-v2/about-service-payments).

            </ResponseField>

          </Expandable>

        </ResponseField>

        <ResponseField name="pricingPlanIds" type="string[]">

          IDs of pricing plans that can be used as payment for the service.
          Read more about [service payment options](https://dev.wix.com/docs/api-reference/business-solutions/bookings/services/services-v2/about-service-payments).

          **Read-only.** Constraints: `format GUID`, `maxItems 75`.

        </ResponseField>

        <ResponseField name="addOnOption" type="string">

          How customers can pay for add-ons when paying for the related booking with a [pricing plan](https://dev.wix.com/docs/api-reference/business-solutions/pricing-plans/pricing-plans/introduction).
          If customers pay for the booking using any method other than a pricing plan, the value of this field is ignored.

          **Supported values:**

          - `ONLINE` — Customers are required to pay online for add-ons when the booking is paid for with a pricing plan.
          - `IN_PERSON` — Customers are required to pay in person for add-ons when the booking is paid for with a pricing plan.

        </ResponseField>

        <ResponseField name="discountInfo" type="DiscountInfo">

          Estimated discount information for the service based on active [eCommerce discounts](https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/e-commerce/extensions/discounts/introduction).
          The final discount is determined during eCommerce checkout and may differ from the estimate,
          for example when discounts depend on cart totals.

          A discount is considered active when its start time has passed and its end time hasn't.
          If multiple active discounts apply, the most recently created one is returned.

          Returned only when `DISCOUNT_INFO_DETAILS` is requested.

          **Read-only.**

          <Expandable title="discountInfo properties">

            <ResponseField name="discountName" type="string">

              Name of the discount. For example, `Summer Sale - 20% Off`.

              Constraints: `minLength 1`, `maxLength 50`.

            </ResponseField>

            <ResponseField name="priceAfterDiscount" type="Money">

              Estimated price after applying the discount. The final price is determined at checkout and may differ when additional discounts are applied or rules are re-evaluated with complete booking information.

              Not returned when the discount depends on booking or cart context, for example a discount that applies to a service only when booked together with another service, or when the discount requires information only available at checkout.

              <Expandable title="priceAfterDiscount properties">

                <ResponseField name="value" type="string">

                  Monetary amount. Decimal string with a period as a decimal separator. For example `25.05`.

                  Constraints: `format DECIMAL_VALUE`.

                </ResponseField>

                <ResponseField name="currency" type="string">

                  Currency code in [ISO 4217 format](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISO_4217). For example, `USD`.

                  **Read-only.** Constraints: `format CURRENCY`.

                </ResponseField>

                <ResponseField name="formattedValue" type="string">

                  Monetary amount. Decimal string in local format. For example, `1 000,30`.

                </ResponseField>

              </Expandable>

            </ResponseField>

          </Expandable>

        </ResponseField>

      </Expandable>

              **Developer Preview.**

              <Expandable title="subscription properties">

                <ResponseField name="amountPerBillingCycle" type="Money">

                  Base price charged per billing cycle before tax and discounts.

                </ResponseField>

                <ResponseField name="frequency" type="string">

                  Billing frequency for recurring subscription charges.
                  Reflected as `billingFrequency` in the booking's subscription info.

                  Currently supports `MONTHLY` only.

                  **Supported values:**

                  - `MONTHLY` — Billing cycle repeats once per month.

                </ResponseField>

                <ResponseField name="totalPayments" type="integer">

                  Total number of billing cycles in the subscription. For example, `12` for a 12-month subscription with monthly payments.
                  Reflected as `totalCycles` in the booking's subscription info.

                  Required when: `rateType` is `SUBSCRIPTION`.

                </ResponseField>

                <ResponseField name="firstChargeDate" type="string">

                  Determines when the first billing cycle payment is charged. Used together with `recurringStartDate` to define the subscription billing schedule.

                  **Supported values:**

                  - `CHECKOUT` — The first billing cycle is charged at checkout. Recurring billing continues from the `recurringStartDate`.
                  - `SCHEDULED` — The first billing cycle is charged on the `recurringStartDate`.

                </ResponseField>

                <ResponseField name="recurringStartDate" type="datetime">

                  Date and time when recurring billing starts.

                  When `firstChargeDate` is `CHECKOUT`, the first charge occurs at checkout and subsequent billing cycles start from this date.

                  When `firstChargeDate` is `SCHEDULED`, the first charge occurs on this date.

                  Customers who book after recurring billing starts are billed for the remaining billing cycles only.

                  Required when: `rateType` is `SUBSCRIPTION`.

                </ResponseField>
