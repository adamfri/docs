    Retrieves daily performance metrics for a location.

    Covers how people found and used the profile: impressions on Google Search
    and Maps split by desktop and mobile, and actions such as calls, direction
    requests, website clicks, messages, and bookings.

    Specify the metrics you want in `metricTypes` and the window in
    `timeRange`. Each returned metric carries its own daily series, so ask only
    for the ones you'll use.

    <Note>

      **Authentication**

      You can only call this method when authenticated as a Wix app or Wix user [identity](https://dev.wix.com/docs/api-reference/articles/authentication/about-identities).

    </Note>

    ## Permissions

    This method requires one of the following permission scopes:

    <div className="wix-scopes">

    <span className="wix-scope" title="SCOPE.PROMOTE.MANAGE-SEO">Manage SEO Settings</span>

    </div>

    Learn more about [app permissions](https://dev.wix.com/docs/build-apps/develop-your-app/access/authorization/about-permissions).

    Callable by: `APP`.

    ## Endpoint

    ```http
    GET https://www.wixapis.com/gbp/v1/locations/{locationId}/insights
    ```

    ## Path Params

    <ParamField path="locationId" type="string" required>

      Location ID. This is Google's location identifier.

    </ParamField>

    ## Query Params

    <ParamField query="timeRange" type="InsightsTimeRange" required>

      Time range for the insights query.

      <Expandable title="timeRange properties">

        <ParamField query="startDate" type="datetime">

          Start of the time range (inclusive). Truncated to UTC midnight.

        </ParamField>

        <ParamField query="endDate" type="datetime">

          End of the time range (inclusive). Truncated to UTC midnight.

        </ParamField>

      </Expandable>

    </ParamField>

    <ParamField query="metricTypes" type="string[]" required>

      Daily metrics to fetch. One InsightsMetric is returned per requested metric.
      Bounded by the number of DailyMetric values.

      Constraints: `maxItems 11`.

      **Supported values:**

      - `BUSINESS_IMPRESSIONS_DESKTOP_MAPS` — Business impressions on Google Maps from desktop devices.
      - `BUSINESS_IMPRESSIONS_DESKTOP_SEARCH` — Business impressions on Google Search from desktop devices.
      - `BUSINESS_IMPRESSIONS_MOBILE_MAPS` — Business impressions on Google Maps from mobile devices.
      - `BUSINESS_IMPRESSIONS_MOBILE_SEARCH` — Business impressions on Google Search from mobile devices.
      - `BUSINESS_CONVERSATIONS` — Message conversations received on the business profile.
      - `BUSINESS_DIRECTION_REQUESTS` — Direction requests to the business location.
      - `CALL_CLICKS` — Clicks on the business profile call button.
      - `WEBSITE_CLICKS` — Clicks on the business profile website link.
      - `BUSINESS_BOOKINGS` — Bookings made via Reserve with Google.
      - `BUSINESS_FOOD_ORDERS` — Food orders received from the business profile.
      - `BUSINESS_FOOD_MENU_CLICKS` — Clicks on menu content shown on the business profile.

    </ParamField>

    ## Response Object

    <ResponseField name="metrics" type="InsightsMetric[]">

      Performance metrics for the location over the requested time range.

      <Expandable title="metrics properties">

        <ResponseField name="metricType" type="string">

          The metric this breakdown is for.

          **Supported values:**

          - `BUSINESS_IMPRESSIONS_DESKTOP_MAPS` — Business impressions on Google Maps from desktop devices.

          - `BUSINESS_IMPRESSIONS_MOBILE_MAPS` — Business impressions on Google Maps from mobile devices.
          - `BUSINESS_IMPRESSIONS_MOBILE_SEARCH` — Business impressions on Google Search from mobile devices.
          - `BUSINESS_CONVERSATIONS` — Message conversations received on the business profile.
          - `BUSINESS_DIRECTION_REQUESTS` — Direction requests to the business location.
          - `CALL_CLICKS` — Clicks on the business profile call button.
          - `WEBSITE_CLICKS` — Clicks on the business profile website link.
          - `BUSINESS_BOOKINGS` — Bookings made via Reserve with Google.
          - `BUSINESS_FOOD_ORDERS` — Food orders received from the business profile.
          - `BUSINESS_FOOD_MENU_CLICKS` — Clicks on menu content shown on the business profile.

        </ResponseField>

        <ResponseField name="totalValue" type="string">

          Total value for the requested time range.

        </ResponseField>

        <ResponseField name="dailyValues" type="DailyMetricValue[]">

          Daily breakdown of the metric.

          Constraints: `maxItems 366`.

          <Expandable title="dailyValues properties">

            <ResponseField name="date" type="datetime">

              Date for this data point. Truncated to UTC midnight.

            </ResponseField>

            <ResponseField name="value" type="string">

              Value for this day.

            </ResponseField>

          </Expandable>

        </ResponseField>

      </Expandable>

    </ResponseField>
