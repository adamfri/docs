With the Members API you can provide site members with a personal account area for social communities or non-social businesses. People who sign up to the Members Area get their own account where they can see all their information while getting to know other members. Members can choose if their account is private or public and set up their profile.

With the Members API you can:

- [Create](https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/crm/members-contacts/members/members/create-member), [delete](https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/crm/members-contacts/members/members/delete-member), [update](https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/crm/members-contacts/members/members/update-member) a member.
- [Get](https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/crm/members-contacts/members/members/get-member) information about a member.
- Allow a current member to [join](https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/crm/members-contacts/members/members/join-community) or [leave](https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/crm/members-contacts/members/members/leave-community) a community.
- Restrict abusive members by [muting](https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/crm/members-contacts/members/members/mute-member) or [blocking](https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/crm/members-contacts/members/members/block-member) them.

## Before you begin

- A site must have the [Wix Members Area](fallback::https://www.wix.com/app-market/web-solution/members-area) installed from the App Market.

## Member Types

There are 6 member types:
- **Private**: Only a Wix user can see this member.
- **Public**: Anyone can see this member.
- **Blocked**: A member that is suspended from entering the Members Area.
- **Muted**: A member that can't engage with the community. For example, they can't write posts or like comments.
- **Active**: A member that can engage with the community. For example, they can write posts and like comments.
- **Pending**: A member that requested to join and isn't approved yet.

## Terminology

- **Member:** An individual who has signed up for the Members Area of a Wix site. Members can interact with the site and other members, depending on their access and activity status. When you create a member, a [contact](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/api-reference/crm/members-contacts/contacts/contacts/contact-v4/introduction) is also created. Wix users can view and manage members and contacts in the site dashboard under **Customers & Leads > Contacts**.
- **Member area:** A tool to create a community around a site to engage a site's visitors. See [About the Members Area](fallback::https://support.wix.com/en/article/site-members-about-the-members-area) for more information. 
- **Profile:** The visible representation of a member, which includes display details such as name, image, and other customizable fields.
- **Community:** A group of members who interact with each other within the site’s social features, such as forums, blogs, or discussion boards.
