Wix Headless lets you keep Wix's business solutions — stores, bookings, events, members —
while building and hosting the frontend yourself, in any framework.

<Note>
  This section is a sample port of
  [`headless-docs/authentication`](https://dev.wix.com/docs/go-headless/getting-started/setup/authentication/about-authentication)
  from `wix-private/developer-docs`, converted to Mintlify MDX by the POC generator. It
  demonstrates that all-markdown knowledge-base content and generated API reference content
  can live in the same Mintlify instance.
</Note>
