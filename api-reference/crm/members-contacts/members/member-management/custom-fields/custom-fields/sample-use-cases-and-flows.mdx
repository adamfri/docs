## Dynamic member profile customization based on subscription tier

In this scenario, a membership-based platform offers different profile customization options depending on the member’s subscription tier:

|Subscription Tier|Custom Profile Fields|
|-----------------|------------------------|
|Free Members       |Bio, Location           |
|Premium Members    |Bio, Location, Job Title, Website |
|Enterprise Members |Bio, Location, Job Title, Website, Company Name, LinkedIn Profile, Industry |

When a member upgrades or downgrades the subscription, their profile fields should update dynamically.

To customize profiles depending on the subscription level:

1. Call [Create Custom Field](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/crm/members-contacts/members/custom-fields/custom-fields/create-custom-field) to create the custom fields above.
1. Call [Create Custom Field Application](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/crm/members-contacts/members/custom-fields/custom-field-applications/create-custom-field-application) to apply the custom fields to members with appropriate subscriptions.
1. When a member upgrades or downgrades to a subscription, call [Get Members Custom Field Applications](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/crm/members-contacts/members/custom-fields/custom-field-applications/get-members-custom-field-applications) to see the member's current fields.
1. Compare the current custom fields with the ones from the higher or lower subscription level.
1. Add or remove missing custom fields from the member by calling [Update Custom Field Application](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/crm/members-contacts/members/custom-fields/custom-field-applications/update-custom-field-application).
