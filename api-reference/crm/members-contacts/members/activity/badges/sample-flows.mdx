This article presents a possible use case and a corresponding sample flow that you can support. This can be a helpful jumping off point as you plan your implementation.

## Assign badges for the most active members

You can assign badges to members based on how many bookings they have in the past 6 months.

1. Call [List Members](https://dev.wix.com/docs/sdk/backend-modules/members/members/list-members) to retrieve a full list of a site's members and their contact IDs.
1. Call [Query Extended Bookings](https://dev.wix.com/docs/sdk/backend-modules/bookings/extended-bookings/query-extended-bookings) filtered by a `startDate` of 6 months ago and sort by `contactDetails.contactId`. Count the amount of bookings per contact and save the specific members with the amount of bookings to earn the badge.
1. Call [Create Badge](https://dev.wix.com/docs/sdk/backend-modules/members/badges/create-badge) to create a badge for members with the most bookings.
1. Call [Assign Badge](https://dev.wix.com/docs/sdk/backend-modules/members/badges/assign-badge) to assign the badge to these specific members.
