    Retrieves full product details from the marketplace provider and adds them to the site owner's Wix Stores catalog.

    Wix calls this method when a site owner selects products to import from the marketplace provider.

    Wix calls this method to create products in the site's Wix Stores catalog, linked to the provider's product IDs.

    ## Endpoint

    ```http
    POST /v2/bulk/add-products-to-store
    ```

    ## Body Params

    <ParamField body="productReferences" type="ProductReference[]">

      Product references to add to the site owner's store.

      Constraints: `minItems 1`, `maxItems 100`.

      <Expandable title="productReferences properties">

        <ParamField body="providerProductId" type="string">

          Unique product identifier on the provider's platform.

          Constraints: `minLength 1`, `maxLength 36`.

        </ParamField>

        <ParamField body="options" type="object">

          Additional provider-specific data for this product. For example, other internal identifiers of the product on the provider's side.

        </ParamField>

        <ParamField body="visibleInStore" type="boolean">

          Whether the product is visible in the site's store.

          Default: `false`

        </ParamField>

      </Expandable>

    </ParamField>
