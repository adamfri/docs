With the Staff Members API you can create and manage event staff. Depending on the provided permissions, the staff members can handle guest check-ins, ticket scanning, add guests manually to an event, track payments, and see event analytics. After creating a staff member they get a unique direct link to access the [Check-in app](fallback::https://support.wix.com/en/article/check-in-by-wix-downloading-the-app) and manage events. This app activates the staff member and checks the authorization for the specific activities.

With the Staff Members API you can:

- [Create](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/events/staff-members/create-staff-member) a staff member by assigning all or specific events for them to access.
- [Delete](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/events/staff-members/delete-staff-member) a staff member.
- [Update](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/events/staff-members/update-staff-member) a staff member.
- [Retrieve](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/events/staff-members/query-staff-members) information about staff members.

## Before you begin

- A site must have [Wix Events & Tickets](fallback::https://www.wix.com/app-market/web-solution/events) installed.

## Use Cases

- [Build a staff management and check-in system for multi-day conferences](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/events/staff-members/sample-use-cases-and-flows)

## Terminology

- **Staff Member**: A person associated with a Wix event who can be assigned various roles and responsibilities.
- **Login Token**: Tokens used for joining as a staff member or logging into an event as an authorized staff member.
