    Retrieves sender details.

    The sender details include the information displayed as the email sender's name and email address.

    + If you send an email campaign with a public email domain (e.g. @gmail.com or @yahoo.com), the email address will not be displayed in the 'from' header. Instead, the email address is replaced with @wixemails.com (or @wixsitemail.com for free users), and the sender's email is placed in the 'reply-to' header.
    + If you send an email campaign with an email from a Wix managed domain, that email will be displayed in the 'from' header.
    + If you send an email from a custom domain that is not managed by Wix, a line will be inserted that states that the email was sent via wixemails.com.

    <Note>

      **Authentication**

      You can only call this method when authenticated as a Wix app or Wix user [identity](https://dev.wix.com/docs/api-reference/articles/authentication/about-identities).

    </Note>

    ## Permissions

    This method requires one of the following permission scopes:

    <div className="wix-scopes">

    <span className="wix-scope" title="SCOPE.DC-PROMOTE.EMAIL-MARKETING">Manage Email Marketing</span>

    </div>

    Learn more about [app permissions](https://dev.wix.com/docs/build-apps/develop-your-app/access/authorization/about-permissions).

    Callable by: `APP`.

    ## Endpoint

    ```http
    GET https://www.wixapis.com/email-marketing/v1/sender-details
    ```

    ## Response Object

    <ResponseField name="senderDetails" type="SenderDetails">

      Current sender details.

      See the [Sender Details Object](/api-reference/business-management/marketing/emails/email-marketing/sender-details/sender-details-object) for this object's fields.

    </ResponseField>
