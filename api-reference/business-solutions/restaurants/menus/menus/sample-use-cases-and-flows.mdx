## Sync pizza menus across all sites

If you have several pizzeria sites, you might need to sync menus across those sites. For example, if there's a completely new menu called "Build-Your-Own-Pizza", this change also needs to be reflected on other sites.    

To sync the pizza menu across all sites:

1. Call [Get Menu](https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/restaurants/menus/menu/get-menu) on the site where you created a new menu.
1. Extract the whole `menu` object.
1. Call [Create Menu](https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/restaurants/menus/menu/create-menu) for your other sites and pass the new menu to each.
