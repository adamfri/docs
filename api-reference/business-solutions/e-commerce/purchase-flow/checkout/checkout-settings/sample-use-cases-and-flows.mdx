## Update checkout settings across all sites

You can synchronize checkout settings across multiple sites. For example, if the return policies change on 1 site, and now the policies guarantee a 100-day item return, this change can also be reflected on other sites.

To update the checkout policy settings across multiple sites:

1. Call [Get Checkout Settings](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/e-commerce/checkout-settings/get-checkout-settings) on the site where you updated the policy.
1. Extract the `checkoutPolicies.returnPolicy.content` field value.
1. Call [Update Checkout Settings](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/e-commerce/checkout-settings/update-checkout-settings) for other sites and pass the new policy to each.
