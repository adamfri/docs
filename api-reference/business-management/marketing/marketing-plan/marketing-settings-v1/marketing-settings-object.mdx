---
title: "Marketing Settings Object"
description: "A site's marketing preferences that shape how the AI generates its marketing plan and posts."
icon: "cube"
---

A site's marketing preferences that shape how the AI generates its marketing plan and posts.

Settings are optional: if a site hasn't configured them, sensible defaults are applied
automatically when a plan is generated. Owners can customize any combination of fields
using the UpsertMarketingSettings method. Settings are shared across all enabled marketing
tools unless a tool-specific override is set in `overrides`.

<Tabs>

  <Tab title="REST">

    <ResponseField name="id" type="string">

      Unique ID of the marketing settings, equal to the site's MetaSite ID.

      **Read-only.**

    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="settings" type="Settings">

      Core preferences that apply to all marketing tools unless a tool-specific override is set.

      <Expandable title="settings properties">

        <ResponseField name="toneOfVoice" type="string">

          Writing style description used to shape the voice of generated post captions.

        </ResponseField>

        <ResponseField name="pointOfView" type="string">

          Narrative perspective used in generated post captions (first, second, or third person).

          **Supported values:**

          - `FIRST_PERSON` — Uses "I", "we", "my", "our".
          - `SECOND_PERSON` — Uses "you", "your".
          - `THIRD_PERSON` — Uses "they", "their", or the business name.

        </ResponseField>

        <ResponseField name="customContentGuidelines" type="string">

          Free-text instructions that further personalize how content is written, beyond `tone_of_voice`.

        </ResponseField>

        <ResponseField name="language" type="Language">

          Language and regional variation used for generated content. Read-only; derived from site properties.

          **Read-only.**

          <Expandable title="language properties">

            <ResponseField name="language" type="string">

              Language for generated content (e.g., `English`, `Spanish`).

            </ResponseField>

            <ResponseField name="regionalVariation" type="string">

              Regional variation of the language (e.g., `Australian` when `language` is `English`).

            </ResponseField>

          </Expandable>

        </ResponseField>

        <ResponseField name="topics" type="Topics">

          Content pillars: topics the AI should focus on or avoid when generating posts.

          <Expandable title="topics properties">

            <ResponseField name="included" type="string[]">

              Topics the AI should actively incorporate into generated content.

              Constraints: `maxLength 50`, `minItems 0`, `maxItems 50`.

            </ResponseField>

            <ResponseField name="excluded" type="string[]">

              Topics the AI should avoid in generated content.

              Constraints: `maxLength 50`, `minItems 0`, `maxItems 50`.

            </ResponseField>

            <ResponseField name="coreTopic" type="string">

              The primary theme that anchors all generated content. Stored but not currently used during
              generation; the fields that influence generation are `topics.included` and `topics.excluded`.

            </ResponseField>

          </Expandable>

        </ResponseField>

        <ResponseField name="imageGuidelines" type="string">

          Free-text style instructions applied when generating images for posts.

        </ResponseField>

        <ResponseField name="marketingPlanGoal" type="MarketingPlanGoal">

          Overall goal of the marketing plan, used to align content strategy across all tools.

          <Expandable title="One of the following">

            <ResponseField name="goal" type="goal">

              goal

              <Expandable title="goal properties">

                <ResponseField name="goalKey" type="string">

                  A standard goal selected from the available plan goal options.

                  **Supported values:**

                  - `DRIVE_TRAFFIC` — Increase visits to the site.
                  - `BRAND_AWARENESS` — Grow recognition and reach of the brand.
                  - `BOOST_SALES` — Promote products or services to increase purchases.
                  - `COLLECT_LEADS` — Capture contact information from potential customers.

                </ResponseField>

                <ResponseField name="customGoal" type="string">

                  A free-text goal provided when none of the standard options apply.

                </ResponseField>

              </Expandable>

            </ResponseField>

          </Expandable>

        </ResponseField>

        <ResponseField name="socialChannels" type="string[]">

          Social platforms to generate content for. Social post drafts are only produced when
          `SOCIAL_MARKETING` is included in `marketing_tools`.

          Constraints: `minItems 0`, `maxItems 10`.

          **Supported values:**

          - `FACEBOOK` — Facebook.
          - `INSTAGRAM` — Instagram.
          - `LINKEDIN` — LinkedIn.
          - `TWITTER` — Deprecated: No longer functional as of July 31, 2026.
          - `PINTEREST` — Pinterest.
          - `GBP` — Google Business Profile.
          - `TIKTOK` — TikTok.

        </ResponseField>

        <ResponseField name="frequency" type="Frequency">

          Posting cadence that acts as a fallback for social marketing only. Per-tool cadences are
          controlled by `overrides.socialMarketing.frequency`, `overrides.blogs.frequency`, and
          `overrides.emailMarketing.frequency` (each with its own per-tool default).

          <Expandable title="frequency properties">

            <ResponseField name="interval" type="string">

              Time unit for the posting cadence (`WEEK`, `MONTH`, or `YEAR`).

              **Supported values:**

              - `WEEK` — Per-week cadence.
              - `MONTH` — Per-month cadence.
              - `YEAR` — Per-year cadence.

            </ResponseField>

            <ResponseField name="count" type="integer">

              Number of posts to produce within each `interval`.

            </ResponseField>

          </Expandable>

        </ResponseField>

        <ResponseField name="marketingTools" type="string[]">

          Marketing tools enabled in the plan. Social post drafts are only generated when
          `SOCIAL_MARKETING` is present.

          Constraints: `minItems 0`, `maxItems 3`.

          **Supported values:**

          - `EMAIL_MARKETING` — Email marketing campaigns.
          - `BLOGS` — Blog post creation.
          - `SOCIAL_MARKETING` — Social media post drafts. Must be enabled for social post drafts to be generated.

        </ResponseField>

            <ResponseField name="targetAudience" type="string">

              Description of the intended audience the business wants to reach.

            </ResponseField>

          </Expandable>

        </ResponseField>

              Constraints: `format GUID`, `minItems 0`, `maxItems 20`.

              List of service areas where the business operates (maximum 5)

              </Expandable>

            </ResponseField>

              Indicates whether the business operates online

            <ResponseField name="updatedDate" type="string">

              Timestamp when the business location was last updated (ISO 8601 format)

              **Read-only.** Constraints: `format LOCAL_DATE_TIME`.

            </ResponseField>

          </Expandable>

        </ResponseField>

        <ResponseField name="imageGenerationSettings" type="ImageGenerationSettings">

          Controls how images are generated for posts (mode and photo sources). These values are stored
          but aren't currently applied during generation.

          <Expandable title="imageGenerationSettings properties">

            <ResponseField name="mode" type="string">

              How images are generated for posts.

              **Supported values:**

              - `RAW` — Uses existing photos from enabled sources without AI processing.
              - `AI_ONLY` — Generates images entirely with AI, without using existing photos.
              - `REMIX` — Applies AI enhancements to existing photos from enabled sources.

            </ResponseField>

            <ResponseField name="enabledPhotoSources" type="string[]">

              Photo sources the AI may draw from when generating or remixing images.

              Constraints: `minItems 0`, `maxItems 2`.

              **Supported values:**

              - `PRODUCTS_CATALOG` — Photos from the site's product catalog.
              - `SERVICES_CATALOG` — Photos from the site's services catalog.

            </ResponseField>

          </Expandable>

        </ResponseField>

        <ResponseField name="userPreferences" type="string">

          User-authored preferences for personalizing marketing output. This value belongs to the site
          and is shared by collaborators; it is not scoped to an individual user.

        </ResponseField>

      </Expandable>

    </ResponseField>
