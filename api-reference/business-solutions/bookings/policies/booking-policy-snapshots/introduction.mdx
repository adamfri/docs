A booking policy snapshot is a saved version of a service's booking policy at the time a [booking](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/bookings/bookings/about-the-bookings-apis)
is created. This is important because a policy may change after a customer
books the service, and having a snapshot preserves the original terms.

The Booking Policy Snapshots API allows you to retrieve policy snapshots.

For managing cancellation fees, you may also want to explore the [Booking Fees API](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/bookings/pricing/booking-fees/introduction).

## Before You Begin

It’s important to note the following points before starting to code:

- Every booking with corresponding [eCommerce order](https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/e-commerce/orders/introduction)
  has exactly one policy snapshot. Bookings without a related eCommerce order don't have
  a policy snapshot.
- You can't create a booking policy snapshot with the Booking Policy Snapshots
  API, you can only retrieve policy snapshots. However, you could call [Get Booking Policy](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/bookings/policies/booking-policies/get-booking-policy)
  to retrieve a service's booking policy just before or after creating a
  booking.

## Terminology

- **Booking policy**: Rules set by the business owner regarding the booking and
  cancellation of a service. Policies may include advance booking restrictions,
  cancellation deadlines, associated fees, and the maximum number of participants
  allowed.
- **Booking policy snapshot**: The version of a service's booking policy saved
  at the time a booking is created. Snapshots preserve the original terms, which
  is useful if policies change after a booking is made.

For a comprehensive glossary of Wix Bookings terms, see [Terminology](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/bookings/terminology).
