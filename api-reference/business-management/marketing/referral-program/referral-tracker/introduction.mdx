The Referral Tracker API allows you to monitor and analyze referral events in a site's referral program.

With the Referral Tracker API, you can:

+ [Get a referral event](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-management/marketing/referral-program/referral-tracker/get-referral-event) to retrieve details about a specific referral event.
+ [Query referral events](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-management/marketing/referral-program/referral-tracker/query-referral-event) to filter and retrieve multiple referral events.
+ [Get referral statistics](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-management/marketing/referral-program/referral-tracker/get-referral-statistics) to obtain overall metrics about a referral program.

For more information about managing loyalty programs, see the [Loyalty API](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/crm/loyalty-program/introduction). To learn more about referral programs, see [Managing Your Referral Program](fallback::https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-loyalty-program-managing-your-loyalty-program).

## Before you begin

It's important to note the following points before starting to code:

- To use the Referral Tracker API, a site needs to have the Wix Loyalty Program app installed.
- The Referral Tracker API requires a Business or higher plan for Wix Editor, or a Plus or higher plan for Wix Studio.
- A site must have at least one of these apps installed: Wix Stores, Wix Bookings, Wix Events, Wix Pricing Plans, or Wix Restaurants Orders.

## Terminology

- **Referral event**: A record of an action related to a referral program, such as a signup or a successful referral.
- **Referring customer**: A site member who shares a referral URL with potential new customers.
- **Referred friend**: A person who signs up or takes an action on your site after being referred by a referring customer.
- **Referral statistics**: Overall metrics about a referral program's performance.
- **Referral reward**: A benefit given to either the referring customer or the referred friend for participating in a referral program.
- **Trigger**: The specific action that initiates a referral event, such as a signup or a purchase.
- **Reward type**: The type of benefit given for a successful referral, which can be a coupon, loyalty points, or no reward.
