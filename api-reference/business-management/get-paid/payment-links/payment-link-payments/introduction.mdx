Payment Link Payments are details of the payments made using [payment links](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-management/get-paid/payment-links/payment-links/introduction).

The Payment Link Payments API enables you to:
- View payments.
- Issue receipts.

## Use Cases

- [Display all payments for a payment link](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-management/get-paid/payment-links/payment-link-payments/sample-flows#display-all-payments-for-a-payment-link)
- [Issue a receipt for payment](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-management/get-paid/payment-links/payment-link-payments/sample-flows#issue-a-receipt-for-payment)

## Terminology
- **Payment link**: Unique URL that can be sent to customers, allowing them to make convenient online payments for goods and services. Also referred to as pay link.
- **Regular payment link payment**: Any payment made with a payment method that is not a Wix eCommerce gift card. Includes payments made through Wix Payments, Stripe, and all [supported payment gateways](fallback::https://www.wix.com/payments/payment-gateways).
