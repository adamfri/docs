    Retrieves a list of recipients for a campaign, filtered by a specific activity type such as opened, clicked, or bounced.

    Results are paginated with a default of 40 recipients per page and a maximum of 1,000 per page.

    Call [List Statistics](https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/email-marketing/campaign-statistics/list-statistics) to retrieve aggregate statistics for campaigns.
    Call [List Campaigns](https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/email-marketing/campaigns/list-campaigns) to retrieve additional information about campaigns.

    The `activity` field is required.

    <Note>

      **Authentication**

      You can only call this method when authenticated as a Wix app or Wix user [identity](https://dev.wix.com/docs/api-reference/articles/authentication/about-identities).

    </Note>

    ## Permissions

    This method requires one of the following permission scopes:

    <div className="wix-scopes">

    <span className="wix-scope" title="SCOPE.DC-PROMOTE.READ-EMAIL-MARKETING">Read Email Marketing</span>

    </div>

    Learn more about [app permissions](https://dev.wix.com/docs/build-apps/develop-your-app/access/authorization/about-permissions).

    Callable by: `APP`.

    ## Endpoint

    ```http
    GET https://www.wixapis.com/email-marketing/v1/campaigns/{campaignId}/statistics/recipients
    ```

    ## Path Params

    <ParamField path="campaignId" type="string" required>

      Campaign ID.

    </ParamField>

    ## Query Params

    <ParamField query="activity" type="string" required>

      Recipient activity type to filter by.

      **Supported values:**

      - `DELIVERED` — Email was successfully delivered to the recipient.
      - `OPENED` — Recipient opened the email.
      - `CLICKED` — Recipient clicked a link in the email.
      - `BOUNCED` — Email bounced and wasn't delivered to the recipient.
      - `NOT_SENT` — Email wasn't sent due to campaign termination.
      - `SENT` — Email was sent to the recipient (includes delivered and bounced).
      - `NOT_OPENED` — Email was delivered but the recipient didn't open it.

    </ParamField>

    <ParamField query="paging" type="CursorPaging">

      Pagination options.

      <Expandable title="paging properties">

        <ParamField query="limit" type="integer">

          Number of items to load.

        </ParamField>

        <ParamField query="cursor" type="string">

          Pointer to the next or previous page in the list of results.

          You can get the relevant cursor token
          from the `pagingMetadata` object in the previous call's response.
          Not relevant for the first request.

        </ParamField>

      </Expandable>

    </ParamField>
