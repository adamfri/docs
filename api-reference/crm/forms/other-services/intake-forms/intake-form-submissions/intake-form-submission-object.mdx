---
title: "Intake Form Submission Object"
description: "A record representing a contact's completion of an intake form."
icon: "cube"
---

A record representing a contact's completion of an intake form.
Each submission has one of these statuses: `VALID`, `EXPIRED`, `CANCELED`, `EXTENDED`, or `EXEMPT`, with expiration and lifecycle management.

<Tabs>

  <Tab title="REST">

    <ResponseField name="id" type="string">

      Intake form submission ID. Identical to the [Wix Forms form submission ID](https://dev.wix.com/docs/api-reference/crm/forms/form-submissions/submission-object).

      **Read-only.**

    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="createdDate" type="datetime">

      Date and time the intake form submission was created in UTC datetime `YYYY-MM-DDThh:mm:ssZ` format.

      **Read-only.**

    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="revision" type="integer">

      Revision number, which increments by 1 each time the intake form submission is updated.
      To prevent conflicting changes, you must specify the current revision when updating the intake form submission.

      **Read-only.**

    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="signer" type="Signer">

      Information about the person who submitted the intake form.

      <Expandable title="signer properties">

        <ResponseField name="contactId" type="string">

          [Contact](https://dev.wix.com/docs/api-reference/crm/members-contacts/contacts/contacts/introduction) ID.

          **Read-only.**

        </ResponseField>

        <ResponseField name="fullName" type="string">

          Contact's full name.

          **Read-only.**

        </ResponseField>

        <ResponseField name="email" type="string">

          Contact's email address.

          **Read-only.**

        </ResponseField>

        <ResponseField name="submitter" type="string">

          Whether the form was submitted by the customer directly or by the business on the customer's behalf.

          **Read-only.**

          **Supported values:**

          - `CUSTOMER` — Customer submitted the form themselves.
          - `BUSINESS` — Business owner or staff submitted the form on behalf of the customer.

        </ResponseField>

      </Expandable>

    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="status" type="string">

      Submission status.

      **Read-only.**

      **Supported values:**

      - `VALID` — Submission is valid and hasn't expired.
      - `EXPIRED` — Submission has expired. The business must extend the submission or the contact must resubmit the form.
      - `CANCELED` — Submission was canceled. The contact must resubmit the form.
      - `EXTENDED` — The business has manually extended the submission's validity.
      - `EXEMPT` — The business has manually exempted the customer from submitting the form.

    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="expirationDate" type="datetime">

      Date and time when the submission expires in UTC datetime `YYYY-MM-DDThh:mm:ssZ` format.
      Calculated from `createdDate` plus the intake form's `expirationPeriodInMonths`.
      You can manually extend the expiration date.

      **Read-only.**

    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="intakeFormInfo" type="IntakeFormInfo">

      Intake form information.

      Returned only when `INTAKE_FORM_INFO` is requested in the `fields` parameter.

      <Expandable title="intakeFormInfo properties">

        <ResponseField name="id" type="string">

          Intake form ID.

        </ResponseField>

        <ResponseField name="name" type="string">

          Intake form name.

          **Read-only.**

        </ResponseField>

        <ResponseField name="expirationPeriodInMonths" type="integer">

          Expiration period in months for the intake form.

          **Read-only.**

        </ResponseField>

        <ResponseField name="archived" type="boolean">

          Whether the intake form is archived.

          **Read-only.**

        </ResponseField>

      </Expandable>

    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="updatedDate" type="datetime">

      Date and time the submission status was manually updated (canceled or extended) in UTC datetime `YYYY-MM-DDThh:mm:ssZ` format.

      **Read-only.**

    </ResponseField>

    ## Example

    <CodeGroup>

      ```json Object
      {
        "intakeFormSubmission": {
          "id": "880e8400-e29b-41d4-a716-446655440003",
          "createdDate": "2024-01-20T14:30:00.000Z",
          "revision": 1,
          "signer": {
            "contactId": "770e8400-e29b-41d4-a716-446655440002",
            "fullName": "John Doe",
            "email": "john.doe@example.com",
            "submitter": "CUSTOMER"
          },
          "status": "VALID",
          "expirationDate": "2025-01-20T14:30:00.000Z",
          "updatedDate": "2024-01-20T14:30:00.000Z"
        }
      }
      ```

    </CodeGroup>
