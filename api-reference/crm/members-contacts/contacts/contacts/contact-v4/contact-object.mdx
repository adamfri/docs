          Contact's email addresses.

          <Expandable title="emails properties">

            <ResponseField name="items" type="ContactEmail[]">

              List of up to 50 email addresses.

              Constraints: `maxItems 50`.

              <Expandable title="items properties">

                <ResponseField name="id" type="string">

                  Email ID.

                  **Read-only.**

                </ResponseField>

                <ResponseField name="tag" type="string">

                  Email type.

                  `UNTAGGED` is shown as "Other" in the Contact List.

                  **Supported values:**

                  - `UNTAGGED`
                  - `MAIN`
                  - `HOME`
                  - `WORK`

                </ResponseField>

                <ResponseField name="email" type="string">

                  Email address.

                  Constraints: `minLength 1`, `maxLength 320`.

                </ResponseField>

                <ResponseField name="primary" type="boolean">

                  Indicates whether this is the contact's primary email address.
                  When changing `primary` to `true` for an email,
                  the contact's other emails become `false`.
                  Changing the primary email of a contact also affects the subscription status to marketing emails that are decided based on the primary email.

                </ResponseField>

              </Expandable>

            </ResponseField>

          </Expandable>

    <ResponseField name="primaryEmail" type="PrimaryEmail">

      Contact's primary email details.

      **Read-only.**

      <Expandable title="primaryEmail properties">

        <ResponseField name="email" type="string">

          Primary email address.

          This field reflects the email address in `info.emails`
          where `primary` is `true`.

          **Read-only.**

        </ResponseField>

        <ResponseField name="subscriptionStatus" type="string">

          Indicates the recipient's opt-in or opt-out status for marketing emails.

          Default: `NOT_SET`

          **Read-only.**

          **Supported values:**

          - `NO_SUBSCRIPTION_STATUS` — No status exists.
          - `NOT_SET` — No status specified.
          - `PENDING` — Subscription confirmation was requested, but recipient hasn't confirmed yet.
          - `SUBSCRIBED` — Recipient has opted in to marketing emails or SMS messages.
          - `UNSUBSCRIBED` — Recipient has opted out of marketing emails or SMS messages.

        </ResponseField>

        <ResponseField name="deliverabilityStatus" type="string">

          Indicates last reported status of sent emails.

          Default: `NOT_SET`

          **Read-only.**

          **Supported values:**

          - `NOT_SET` — No status reported.
          - `VALID` — Emails are being successfully delivered.
          - `BOUNCED` — The last email to the recipient bounced or was rejected.
          - `SPAM_COMPLAINT` — Recipient registered a spam complaint with their email provider.
          - `INACTIVE` — Multiple emails have been delivered without any kind of engagement from the recipient.

        </ResponseField>

      </Expandable>

    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="primaryPhone" type="PrimaryPhone">

      Contact's primary phone details.

      **Read-only.**

      <Expandable title="primaryPhone properties">

        <ResponseField name="countryCode" type="string">

          Country code of the primary phone number in [ISO-3166 alpha-2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISO_3166-1_alpha-2) format.

        </ResponseField>

        <ResponseField name="e164Phone" type="string">

          Phone number in [ITU E.164](https://www.itu.int/rec/T-REC-E.164/) format.
          Automatically generated using `phone` and `countryCode` when both values are valid.

          **Read-only.**

        </ResponseField>

        <ResponseField name="formattedPhone" type="string">

          Formatted phone. Automatically generated using `phone` and `countryCode`.

          **Read-only.**

        </ResponseField>

        <ResponseField name="subscriptionStatus" type="string">

          Indicates the recipient's opt-in or opt-out status for SMS messages.

          Default:
          - `NOT_SET` when the phone is a valid E164 phone.
          - `NO_SUBSCRIPTION_STATUS` when the phone is not a valid E164 phone.

          **Read-only.**

          **Supported values:**

          - `NO_SUBSCRIPTION_STATUS` — No status exists.
          - `NOT_SET` — No status specified.
          - `PENDING` — Subscription confirmation was requested, but recipient hasn't confirmed yet.
          - `SUBSCRIBED` — Recipient has opted in to marketing emails or SMS messages.
          - `UNSUBSCRIBED` — Recipient has opted out of marketing emails or SMS messages.

        </ResponseField>

        <ResponseField name="phone" type="string">

          Primary phone number.

          This field reflects the phone number in `info.phones`
          where `primary` is `true`.

          **Read-only.**

        </ResponseField>

      </Expandable>

    </ResponseField>
