---
title: "Campaign Object"
description: "An email marketing campaign that distributes promotional content to a set of contacts via email and a public landing page."
icon: "cube"
---

An email marketing campaign that distributes promotional content to a set of contacts via email and a public landing page. A campaign tracks engagement statistics such as opens, clicks and bounces after the email is sent.

<Tabs>

  <Tab title="REST">

    <ResponseField name="campaignId" type="string">

      Campaign ID.

      Constraints: `format GUID`.

    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="title" type="string">

      Campaign title. Used to identify the campaign in the dashboard. Defaults to the email subject if not provided.

    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="firstImageUrl" type="string">

      URL of the first image after the logo.

    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="editorType" type="string">

      Editor type that determines the format of `composerDataJson`.

      **Supported values:**

      - `WEB` — Wix classic drag-and-drop email editor. The `composerDataJson` is stored in Wix proprietary JSON format.
      - `MOBILE` — Wix classic mobile-optimized email editor. The `composerDataJson` is stored in a subset of the `WEB` format.
      - `MJML` — AI-powered email editor. The `composerDataJson` is stored in MJML format wrapped in JSON.

    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="status" type="string">

      Campaign status.

      **Supported values:**

      - `ACTIVE` — Campaign is active.
      - `ARCHIVED` — Campaign is archived.
      - `DELETED` — Campaign is deleted.

    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="visibilityStatus" type="string">

      Whether the campaign is a draft, published, or a template.

      **Supported values:**

      - `DRAFT` — Campaign isn't published yet.
      - `PUBLISHED` — Campaign is published.
      - `TEMPLATE` — Campaign is a template.

    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="distributionStatus" type="string">

      Campaign distribution status.

      **Supported values:**

      - `NOT_STARTED` — Campaign isn't distributed to recipients yet.
      - `SCHEDULED` — Campaign is scheduled for distribution.
      - `IN_DETECTION` — Campaign is in screening for malicious content.
      - `IN_MODERATION` — Campaign is in review by the site moderators.
      - `SAMPLING` — A subset of recipients receives the campaign first to check for deliverability issues before sending to the full audience.
      - `SENDING` — Campaign is being sent out to all recipients.
      - `REJECTED` — Campaign was identified as abusive and rejected before sending.
      - `TERMINATED` — Campaign is terminated after a failed sampling test.
      - `DISTRIBUTED` — Campaign is fully distributed to all recipients.
      - `PAUSED` — Scheduled campaign is paused.

    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="publishingData" type="PublishingData">

      Publishing data. The field is returned empty if the campaign isn't published.

      <Expandable title="publishingData properties">

        <ResponseField name="landingPageUrl" type="string">

          Landing page URL.

          Constraints: `format WEB_URL`.

        </ResponseField>

        <ResponseField name="statistics" type="CampaignStatistics">

          Campaign engagement statistics.

          <Expandable title="statistics properties">

            <ResponseField name="landingPage" type="LandingPageStatistics">

              Landing page statistics.

              <Expandable title="landingPage properties">

                <ResponseField name="opened" type="integer">

                  Number of times the landing page was loaded or viewed.

                </ResponseField>

                <ResponseField name="clicked" type="integer">

                  Number of times a link was clicked in a landing page.

                </ResponseField>

              </Expandable>

            </ResponseField>

            <ResponseField name="emailCampaign" type="DistributionStatistics">

              Email distribution statistics.

              <Expandable title="emailCampaign properties">

                <ResponseField name="delivered" type="integer">

                  Number of delivered emails.

                </ResponseField>

                <ResponseField name="opened" type="integer">

                  Number of recipients who opened an email.

                  If the same recipient opened the same email twice, it is counted as a one unique open.

                </ResponseField>

                <ResponseField name="clicked" type="integer">

                  Number of recipients who clicked a link in the email.

                  If the same recipient clicked the same link twice, it is counted as a one unique click.

                  If the same recipient clicked different links, it is counted as a one unique click.

                </ResponseField>

                <ResponseField name="bounced" type="integer">

                  Number of emails that didn't reach the recipient.

                </ResponseField>

                <ResponseField name="complained" type="integer">

                  Number of recipients who reported the email as spam.

                </ResponseField>

                <ResponseField name="notSent" type="integer">

                  Number of audience members who didn't receive the email because the campaign was terminated during sampling before reaching the full audience.

                </ResponseField>

              </Expandable>

            </ResponseField>

            <ResponseField name="total" type="TotalStatistics">

              Combined statistics of landing page and email distribution.

              <Expandable title="total properties">

                <ResponseField name="mailsSent" type="integer">

                  Total number of emails sent.

                </ResponseField>

                <ResponseField name="opened" type="integer">

                  Number of landing page views and opened emails.

                </ResponseField>

                <ResponseField name="clicked" type="integer">

                  Number of landing page and email link clicks.

                </ResponseField>

              </Expandable>

            </ResponseField>

          </Expandable>

        </ResponseField>

        <ResponseField name="datePublished" type="datetime">

          Date and time the campaign was published.

        </ResponseField>

        <ResponseField name="wasResentToNonOpeners" type="boolean">

          Whether the campaign was resent to recipients who didn't open the original email.

        </ResponseField>

      </Expandable>

    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="dateCreated" type="datetime">

      Date and time the campaign was created.

    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="dateUpdated" type="datetime">

      Date and time the campaign was updated.

    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="emailSubject" type="string" deprecated>

      Email subject of the campaign (initially copied from the template).

      **Deprecated.** Use `email_subject` instead. Removal: 2026-09-30. Constraints: `maxLength 1000`.

    </ResponseField>

    ## Example

    <CodeGroup>

      ```json Object
      {
        "campaignId": "c9b491f8-bad0-4d0d-8a1c-c90055d31553",
        "title": "Email subject",
        "firstImageUrl": "//static.wixstatic.com/media/eceb8e_e1f32b0d497447a8898e7abcf01d7d87~mv2_d_5600_3733_s_4_2.jpg",
        "editorType": "WEB",
        "status": "ACTIVE",
        "visibilityStatus": "PUBLISHED",
        "distributionStatus": "DISTRIBUTED",
        "publishingData": {
          "landingPageUrl": "https://www.example.com/so/SHORTCODE",
          "statistics": {
            "landingPage": {
              "opened": 45,
              "clicked": 12
            },
            "emailCampaign": {
              "delivered": 2,
              "opened": 2,
              "clicked": 2
            },
            "total": {
              "mailsSent": 2,
              "opened": 10,
              "clicked": 8
            }
          },
          "datePublished": "2020-02-14T14:28:54Z",
          "wasResentToNonOpeners": false
        },
        "dateCreated": "2020-02-13T11:18:30Z",
        "dateUpdated": "2020-02-14T14:28:19.680596102Z"
      }
      ```

    </CodeGroup>
