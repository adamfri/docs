The back in stock settings object holds information related to the state of collecting back in stock
[notification requests](https://dev.wix.com/docs/api-reference/business-solutions/e-commerce/other-services/back-in-stock/back-in-stock-notifications/introduction).

<Tabs>

  <Tab title="REST">

    <ResponseField name="collectionStates" type="BackInStockCollectionState[]">

      Information about collecting customer requests to receive
      back in stock notifications.

      <Expandable title="collectionStates properties">

        <ResponseField name="appId" type="string">

          ID of the app to receive notification requests for.

          Back in stock notifications only work for [Wix Stores](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-stores-about-wix-stores).
          The `appId` for Wix Stores is `"215238eb-22a5-4c36-9e7b-e7c08025e04e"`.

          Constraints: `format GUID`.

        </ResponseField>

        <ResponseField name="collectingRequests" type="boolean">

          Whether to collect requests for items from this app.

        </ResponseField>

      </Expandable>

    </ResponseField>

  </Tab>
