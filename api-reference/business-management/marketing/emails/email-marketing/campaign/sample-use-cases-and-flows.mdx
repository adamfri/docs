This article presents possible use cases and corresponding sample flows that you can support. It provides a useful starting point as you plan your implementation.

## A/B test email subject lines

A marketing team wants to determine which subject line drives higher open rates before committing to a full send. They run two variants of the same campaign with different subject lines, measure the results, and send the winning variant to the rest of the audience.

> **Prerequisite:** Create and publish two campaigns with different subject lines before running this flow.

To A/B test email subject lines:

1. Call [List Campaigns](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/api-reference/business-management/marketing/emails/email-marketing/campaign/list-campaigns) to retrieve the campaign IDs for both campaigns. Save the IDs for later.
1. Call [List Statistics](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/api-reference/business-management/marketing/emails/email-marketing/campaign/list-statistics) and pass both campaign IDs. Compare the `email.opened` count for each campaign to determine which subject line performed better.
1. Call [List Recipients](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/api-reference/business-management/marketing/emails/email-marketing/campaign/list-recipients) for the winning campaign with `activity` set to `CLICKED` to identify the most engaged recipients.
1. Call [Label Contact](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/crm/members-contacts/contacts/contacts/contact-v4/label-contact) to tag the clicked recipients with a label for future targeting.
1. Call [Reuse Campaign](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/api-reference/business-management/marketing/emails/email-marketing/campaign/reuse-campaign) on the winning campaign to create a draft copy.
1. Call [Publish Campaign](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/api-reference/business-management/marketing/emails/email-marketing/campaign/publish-campaign) with the label ID from step 4 in `emailDistributionOptions.labelIds` to send the refined campaign to the engaged audience.
