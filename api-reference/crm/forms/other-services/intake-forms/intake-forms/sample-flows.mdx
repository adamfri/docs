For additional sample flows that use both the Intake Forms and Intake Form Submissions APIs, see [Intake Forms: Sample Flows](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/api-reference/crm/forms/other-services/intake-forms/sample-flows).

## Share an intake form with customers

You can create an intake form with a custom expiration period and share it directly with customers via email, SMS, or other channels. This is useful when you need customers to complete forms outside of any specific workflow.

To create and share an intake form:

1. Call Wix Forms' [Create Form](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/api-reference/crm/forms/form-schemas/create-form).
   Specify `"namespace": "wix.waivers.forms"` and include at least these `formFields`: `firstName`, `email`, and `signature`.
   Set `pii: true` and `required: true` in `inputOptions` for each of these form fields.
   Save the returned form ID.
1. Call [Update Intake Form Expiration Period](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/api-reference/crm/forms/other-services/intake-forms/intake-forms/update-intake-form-expiration-period) with the form ID.
   Specify your desired `expirationPeriodInMonths`. For example, `12` for annual forms.
1. Call [Create Customer Submission Link](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/api-reference/crm/forms/other-services/intake-forms/intake-forms/create-customer-submission-link) with the intake form ID to generate a shareable URL.
1. Share the generated link with customers via email, SMS, or your preferred communication channel.
