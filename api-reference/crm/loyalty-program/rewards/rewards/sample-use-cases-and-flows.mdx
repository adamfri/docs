This article shares some possible use cases you could support, as well as a sample flow that could support each use case. This can be a helpful jumping off point as you plan your implementation.

## Create seasonal and limited-time reward campaigns

Design time-sensitive reward campaigns that drive urgency and increase customer engagement during specific periods or events.

1. Call [Get Loyalty Program](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/api-reference/crm/loyalty-program/loyalty-program-management/program/get-loyalty-program) to understand your current program configuration and active customer base.
1. Call [Create Reward](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/api-reference/crm/loyalty-program/rewards/rewards/create-reward) for seasonal rewards with free shipping for 250 points.
1. Set the `active` field to `false` initially to prepare rewards before the campaign launch.
1. When ready to launch, call [Update Reward](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/api-reference/crm/loyalty-program/rewards/rewards/update-reward) to set the `active` field to: `true` for all campaign rewards.
1. Call [Query Loyalty Accounts](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/api-reference/crm/loyalty-program/accounts/accounts/query-loyalty-accounts) to identify customers with sufficient points for targeted campaign notifications.
1. After the campaign ends, call [Update Reward](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/api-reference/crm/loyalty-program/rewards/rewards/update-reward) to set the `active` field `false` or [Delete Reward](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/api-reference/crm/loyalty-program/rewards/rewards/delete-reward) to remove limited-time offers.
