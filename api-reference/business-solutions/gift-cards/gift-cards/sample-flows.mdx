This article presents possible use cases and corresponding sample flows that you can support. These can be helpful jumping off points as you plan your implementation.

## Create and send promotional gift cards

You can create gift cards for promotional campaigns and automatically send them to recipients with personalized messages. This allows businesses to run marketing campaigns or provide customer service compensation.

To create and send promotional gift cards:

1. [Create a gift card](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/gift-cards/gift-cards/create-gift-card) with recipient notification settings. Include the sender and recipient information along with a personalized message for the campaign.

2. Call [Send Gift Card Email](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/gift-cards/gift-cards/send-gift-card-email) to deliver the gift card immediately, or schedule it for later delivery by setting the notification date.

3. Track the gift card status using [Get Gift Card](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/gift-cards/gift-cards/get-gift-card) to monitor when it's redeemed.

## Build a customer service gift card management portal

Customer service representatives often need to create, search, and manage gift cards for various support scenarios. This workflow demonstrates building a comprehensive management interface.

### Part 1: Create gift cards for customer service

To create gift cards for customer service scenarios:

1. [Create a gift card](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/gift-cards/gift-cards/create-gift-card) without notification info for immediate customer use. Use a descriptive code that includes a customer service reference.

2. If a custom code was provided during creation, share it with the customer directly. Note that the `code` field in the response is always obfuscated.

3. Record the gift card ID in your customer service system for future reference.

### Part 2: Search and manage existing gift cards

To help customers find and manage their gift cards:

1. Use [List Gift Cards by Email](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/gift-cards/gift-cards/list-gift-cards-by-email) to find gift cards associated with a customer's email address.

2. For more specific searches, use [Search Gift Cards](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/gift-cards/gift-cards/search-gift-cards) with partial code matching by filtering on the code suffix.

3. If a gift card needs to be deactivated due to fraud or customer request, call [Disable Gift Card](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/gift-cards/gift-cards/disable-gift-card).

## Implement gift card lookup and balance checking

Allow customers to check their gift card status and balance using a self-service interface.

To implement gift card lookup:

1. Accept the gift card code from the customer.

2. Call [Query Gift Cards](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/gift-cards/gift-cards/query-gift-cards) and filter on `code` with an exact match against the code the customer gave you. Responses render the code obfuscated, but you can still match on the full code.

3. Display the gift card balance, expiration date, and status to the customer from the results.

## Look up a gift card from its code suffix

A customer who no longer has their full gift card code often still has its last 4 characters, from a confirmation email or the card itself.

To look up a gift card from its code suffix:

1. Accept the last 4 characters of the gift card code from the customer.

2. Call [Search Gift Cards](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/gift-cards/gift-cards/search-gift-cards) to find the gift card by filtering on the code suffix and sorting by creation date.

3. Identify the customer's gift card among the results, for example by its creation date or recipient email address, and display its balance, expiration date, and status.

## Generate gift card usage reports

Create comprehensive reports for business intelligence and financial auditing by aggregating gift card data.

To generate gift card reports:

1. Call [Query Gift Cards](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/gift-cards/gift-cards/query-gift-cards) to retrieve gift cards for a specific time period by filtering on creation date ranges and sorting chronologically.

2. Use [Count Gift Cards](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/gift-cards/gift-cards/count-gift-cards) to get total counts for different gift card categories, such as active gift cards with remaining balances.

3. Aggregate the data to create reports showing total gift card value issued and outstanding balances.

<Tip>

To view or verify the same gift cards in the site dashboard, go to **Sales** and click **Gift Card Sales**.

</Tip>
