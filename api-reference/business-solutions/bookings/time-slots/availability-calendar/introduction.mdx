<Warning>

__Deprecation Notice:__

**Availability Calendar** has been replaced with [Time Slots V2](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/bookings/time-slots/time-slots-v2/introduction) and will be removed on March 31, 2026.

See our Availability Calendar [migration guide](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/bookings/time-slots/availability-calendar/migration-guide) for details.

</Warning>

The Availability Calendar API enables you to check the availability of
appointment slots and class events, allowing for efficient booking and
scheduling for customers. It also automatically handles time zone differences
and Daylight Savings Time (DST).

With the Availability Calendar API, you can:

- Retrieve available slots for an appointment-based service.
- Retrieve available class events.

## Before you begin

It's important to note the following point before starting to code:

Calculating course availability isn't possible with the Availability Calendar API. To calculate course availability, call [Query Extended Bookings](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/bookings/bookings/bookings-reader-v2/query-extended-bookings)
and retrieve all bookings for a specific course. Then, ensure that the
booking's intended `numberOfParticipants` doesn't exceed the course's number
of remaining places.

See this [sample flow](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/bookings/end-to-end-booking-flows#book-a-course) for more details.

## Terminology

- **Availability**: Availability refers to the time slots on an appointment-based
  service's [schedule](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-management/calendar/schedules-v3/introduction)
  that customers can book.
- **Policy violation**: Indicates any violations of [booking policies](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/bookings/policies/booking-policies/introduction),
  such as booking too early or too late.
- **Event**: A booked or reserved period of time for an appointment, class, or
  course. Alternatively, you can consider an event as a specific instance of a
  service occurring at a particular time. Previously, events were also called
  sessions in Wix Bookings APIs. The aggregation of all events for a service
  constitutes the service's schedule. Learn more about [events](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-management/calendar/events-v3/introduction)
  and [schedules](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-management/calendar/schedules-v3/introduction)
  in the [Calendar APIs](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-management/calendar/introduction).
- **Slot**: A time period that customers can book for a service. For
  appointments, it includes all available times for booking. For classes, it
  refers to sessions that are open for booking.

For a comprehensive glossary of Wix Bookings terms, see [Terminology](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/bookings/terminology).
