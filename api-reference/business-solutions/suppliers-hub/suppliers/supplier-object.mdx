title: "Supplier Object"
description: "A supplier is an individual or company that provides products to marketplace providers, enabling them to sell those products to end users."
icon: "cube"
---

A supplier is an individual or company that provides products to marketplace providers, enabling them to sell those products to end users. Suppliers represent the source layer in the marketplace ecosystem, where providers purchase inventory for their catalogs.

Suppliers can operate through different business models, either dropshipping or wholesale, maintain ratings and review counts, and be tagged for organization. Each supplier is managed by a specific provider application, tracked via the app ID.

<Tabs>

  <Tab title="REST">

    <ResponseField name="id" type="string">

      Supplier ID.

      **Read-only.**

    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="revision" type="integer">

      Revision number, which increments by 1 each time the Supplier is updated.
      To prevent conflicting changes,
      the current revision must be passed when updating the Supplier.

      Ignored when creating a Supplier.

      **Read-only.**

    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="createdDate" type="datetime">

      Date and time the Supplier was created.

      **Read-only.**

    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="updatedDate" type="datetime">

      Date and time the Supplier was last updated.

      **Read-only.**

    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="name" type="string">

      Supplier name. Displayed to providers when browsing and selecting suppliers for their product catalogs.

    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="appId" type="string">

      App ID of the provider application responsible for managing the supplier and its products. Automatically set to the ID of the application making the create request.

      **Read-only.**

    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="location" type="Location">

      Supplier location.

      <Expandable title="location properties">

        <ResponseField name="country" type="string">

          2-letter country code in [ISO 3166-1 alpha-2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISO_3166-1) format. For example, `US` for United States or `GB` for United Kingdom.

        </ResponseField>

      </Expandable>

    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="rating" type="string">

      Supplier rating from 1.00 to 5.00. For example, `4.25`. Provider applications manage ratings and should update both `rating` and `reviewCount` together to maintain consistency.

    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="verified" type="boolean">

      Whether the supplier has passed verification processes.

      Default: `false`

    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="types" type="string[]">

      Supplier business model types. `DROPSHIPPING` suppliers ship directly to end customers, while `WHOLESALE` suppliers ship in bulk to the provider who fulfills orders. A supplier can support multiple fulfillment models.

      Constraints: `minItems 1`, `maxItems 2`.

      **Supported values:**

      - `DROPSHIPPING` — Supplier ships products directly to end customers. The provider does not handle physical inventory or fulfillment.
      - `WHOLESALE` — Supplier ships products in bulk to the provider, who then fulfills orders to end customers.

    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="reviewCount" type="integer">

      Number of reviews for the supplier. Provider applications should update both `rating` and `reviewCount` together to maintain consistency.

    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="tags" type="Tags">

      Supplier tags for organization and categorization.

      <Expandable title="tags properties">

        <ResponseField name="privateTags" type="TagList">

          Tags that require an additional permission in order to access them, normally not given to site members or visitors.

          <Expandable title="privateTags properties">

            <ResponseField name="tagIds" type="string[]">

              List of tag IDs.

              Constraints: `maxLength 5`, `maxItems 100`.

            </ResponseField>

          </Expandable>

        </ResponseField>

        <ResponseField name="publicTags" type="TagList">

          Tags that are exposed to anyone who has access to the labeled entity itself, including site members and visitors.

          <Expandable title="publicTags properties">

            <ResponseField name="tagIds" type="string[]">

              List of tag IDs.

              Constraints: `maxLength 5`, `maxItems 100`.

            </ResponseField>

          </Expandable>

        </ResponseField>

      </Expandable>

    </ResponseField>

      ```json Object
      }
      ```
