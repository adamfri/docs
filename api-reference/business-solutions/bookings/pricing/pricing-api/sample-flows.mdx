This article provides sample flows for retrieving booking pricing information using [Preview Price](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/bookings/pricing/pricing-api/preview-price) and [Calculate Price](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/bookings/pricing/pricing-api/calculate-price). Use these flows as a starting point to implement tailored booking experiences.

## Build a custom checkout flow

You can display services and available slots to customers, let them choose their preferred options, then show accurate pricing in your custom checkout before creating the booking.

1. Display a service list to the customer, let them select a service and an available slot. For example, as described in the [appointment end-to-end booking flow](##book-an-appointment*fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/bookings/end-to-end-booking-flows#book-an-appointment).
1. Call [Preview Price](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/bookings/pricing/pricing-api/preview-price) specifying `bookingLineItems` with `serviceId`, `numberOfParticipants`, and `choices` for each variant.
1. Display the pricing breakdown in your custom checkout interface using `priceInfo.calculatedPrice` for the total cost and `priceInfo.bookingLineItems[].pricePerParticipant` for individual line item pricing.
1. Call [Create Booking](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/bookings/bookings/bookings-writer-v2/create-booking) to complete the booking with the selected slot and pricing information.
1. Once the customer completes the checkout, call [Confirm Or Decline Booking](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/bookings/bookings/bookings-writer-v2/confirm-or-decline-booking).
   Set `options.paymentStatus` to `PAID` to update the booking status to `CONFIRMED`.
1. Call [Create Order](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/e-commerce/orders/orders/create-order) with your custom payment details.

## Display booking costs in an external app

To show booking pricing in external apps or 3rd-party systems:

1. Call [Query Extended Booking](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/bookings/bookings/bookings-reader-v2/query-extended-bookings) to retrieve the relevant booking object.
1. Call [Calculate Price](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/bookings/pricing/pricing-api/calculate-price) specifying the complete `booking` object.
1. Use the returned `priceInfo.calculatedPrice` to display accurate booking costs in your external interface. Make sure to handle deposits and non-numerical prices properly:
   - If `priceInfo.deposit` is present, show deposit requirements separately.
   - Handle responses where `priceInfo.priceDescriptionInfo` contains custom pricing text instead of `calculatedPrice` for services with `CUSTOM` rate types.

## Preview duration-based pricing for a duration-range service

For services with a duration range configured, you can preview the price based on the customer's selected time range. This is useful for duration-range services where the customer chooses how long to book.

1. Call [Get Service](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/bookings/services/services-v2/get-service) to retrieve the service and confirm it has a duration range configured in `schedule.availabilityConstraints.durationRange`.
1. Let the customer select their desired start and end times within the allowed duration range.
1. Call [Preview Price](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/bookings/pricing/pricing-api/preview-price) specifying `bookingLineItems` with `serviceId`, `numberOfParticipants`, `localStartDate`, `localEndDate`, and `timeZone`. For example:

    ```json
    {
      "bookingLineItems": [{
        "serviceId": "8e8839e1-5f16-4e82-a3bb-a1db594b29ba",
        "serviceChoices": [{ "numberOfParticipants": 1 }],
        "localStartDate": "2026-03-23T10:00:00",
        "localEndDate": "2026-03-23T13:00:00",
        "timeZone": "America/New_York"
      }]
    }
    ```

1. Display the returned `priceInfo.calculatedPrice` to the customer. The price is calculated based on the duration: per minute for hourly services and per day for daily services.
