<Note>This article applies to the REST API only.</Note>

This article shares basic flows you could support. You're certainly not
limited to these use cases, but they can be a helpful jumping-off point for your
planning. You could add bookings to the business calendar. These
bookings can be for all types of services: appointment-based, classes, and
courses. You can also use all Wix eCommerce-supported
([SDK](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/sdk/backend-modules/ecom/introduction) | [REST](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/e-commerce/introduction))
payment methods, along with
Wix Pricing Plans
([SDK](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/sdk/backend-modules/pricing-plans/plans/introduction) | [REST](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/pricing-plans/pricing-plans/introduction)).

## Book an appointment

You could display available time slots for appointment-based services
to customers, let them choose a slot, create the related booking, and handle the
payment process using Wix eCommerce
([SDK](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/sdk/backend-modules/ecom/introduction) | [REST](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/e-commerce/introduction)).

### Part 1: Book an appointment

1. Call Get Site Properties
   ([SDK](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/sdk/backend-modules/business-tools/site-properties/get-site-properties) | [REST](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-management/site-properties/properties/get-site-properties))
   to retrieve the `timeZone` of the business location.
1. Use Query Services
   ([SDK](https://dev.wix.com/docs/sdk/backend-modules/bookings/services/query-services) | [REST](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/bookings/services/services-v2/query-services))
   and specify `{"type": "APPOINTMENT"}` as a filter to retrieve all appointment-based services.
1. Display all services to the customer and save the ID of the chosen service.
1. Call Query Availability
   ([SDK](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/sdk/backend-modules/bookings/availability-calendar/query-availability) | [REST](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/bookings/time-slots/availability-calendar/query-availability)).
   Make sure to specify the relevant `serviceId`, the business `timezone`,
   `startDate` and `endDate` as filters.
1. Display the available time slots to the customer and let them select their
   preferred `slot`.
1. Call Create Booking
   ([SDK](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/sdk/backend-modules/bookings/bookings/create-booking) | [REST](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/bookings/bookings/bookings-writer-v2/create-booking)).
   Specify `startDate`, `endDate`, `resource` and `location` from the selected slot
   in `booking.bookedEntity.slot`. If the slot's availability hasn't changed
   since calling Query Availability, the function succeeds and the status of
   the booking is `CREATED`.

### Part 2: Handle the payment process with Wix eCommerce ([SDK](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/sdk/backend-modules/ecom/introduction) | [REST](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/e-commerce/introduction))

1. Call Create Checkout
   ([SDK](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/sdk/backend-modules/ecom/checkout/create-checkout) | [REST](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/e-commerce/checkout/create-checkout)).
   Make sure to specify the [Wix Bookings app ID](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/articles/getting-started/wix-business-solutions)
   as `catalogReference.appId` and the relevant booking ID as
   `catalogReference.catalogItemId`. Save the checkout ID and `totalAfterGiftCard.amount`.
1. Retrieve the checkout's URL with
   Get Checkout URL
   ([SDK](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/sdk/backend-modules/ecom/checkout/get-checkout-url) | [REST](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/e-commerce/checkout/get-checkout-url))
   and redirect the customer.
1. Once the customer completes the checkout, Wix eCommerce automatically creates
   an order
   ([SDK](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/sdk/backend-modules/ecom/orders/setup) | [REST](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/e-commerce/orders/introduction))
   and the customer is charged.

Alternatively, you could follow
these steps
([SDK](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/sdk/backend-modules/bookings/bookings/sample-flows#backend-modules_bookings_bookings_part-2-handle-the-payment-process-with-a-custom-checkout) | [REST](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/bookings/sample-booking-flows#part-2-handle-the-payment-process-with-a-custom-checkout))
to handle the payment process with a custom checkout.

## Book a class session

You could display available class sessions to customers, let
them choose the session, create the booking, and handle the payment process
with a custom checkout.

### Part 1: Book a class session:

1. Call Get Site Properties
   ([SDK](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/sdk/backend-modules/business-tools/site-properties/get-site-properties) | [REST](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-management/site-properties/properties/get-site-properties))
   to retrieve the `timeZone` of the business location.
1. Call Query Services
   ([SDK](https://dev.wix.com/docs/sdk/backend-modules/bookings/services/query-services) | [REST](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/bookings/services/services-v2/query-services))
   and specify `{"type": "CLASS"}` as a filter to retrieve all classes.
1. Display all classes to the customer and save the ID of the chosen service.
1. Call Query Availability
   ([SDK](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/sdk/backend-modules/bookings/availability-calendar/query-availability) | [REST](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/bookings/time-slots/availability-calendar/query-availability)).
   Specify the relevant `serviceId`, the required `openSpots`, the business
   `timezone`, `startDate` and `endDate` as filters.
1. Display the retrieved class sessions to the customer and let them select their
   preferred session. Make sure to save the corresponding `availabilityEntries.slot.eventId`.
1. Call Create Booking
   ([SDK](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/sdk/backend-modules/bookings/bookings/create-booking) | [REST](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/bookings/bookings/bookings-writer-v2/create-booking))
   and specify the saved event ID in `booking.bookedEntity.slot.eventId`.
   If the class session's availability hasn't changed since calling
   Query Availability, the call succeeds and the status of the booking is
   `CREATED`.

### Part 2: Handle the payment process with a custom checkout

1. Implement your custom checkout and display the checkout page to the customer.
1. Once the customer completes the checkout, use
   Confirm Or Decline Booking
   ([SDK](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/sdk/backend-modules/bookings/bookings/confirm-or-decline-booking) | [REST](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/bookings/bookings/bookings-writer-v2/confirm-or-decline-booking)).
   Set `options.paymentStatus` to `PAID` to update the booking status to
   `CONFIRMED`.
1. Call Create Order
   ([SDK](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/sdk/backend-modules/ecom/orders/create-order) | [REST](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/e-commerce/orders/create-order))
   with your custom payment details.

Alternatively, you could follow
these steps
([SDK](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/sdk/backend-modules/bookings/bookings/sample-flows#backend-modules_bookings_bookings_part-2-handle-the-payment-process-with-wix-ecommerce) | [REST](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/bookings/sample-booking-flows#part-2-handle-the-payment-process-with-wix-ecommerce))
to handle the payment process with Wix eCommerce.
## Book a course

You could display available courses to customers, let them choose a
service, and create the booking.

To book a course:

1. Call Query Services
   ([SDK](https://dev.wix.com/docs/sdk/backend-modules/bookings/services/query-services) | [REST](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/bookings/services/services-v2/query-services))
   and specify `{"type": "COURSE"}` as a filter to retrieve all courses.
1. Display all courses to the customer and save the ID of the chosen service
   and its `defaultCapacity`.
1. Retrieve all bookings for this service with
   Query Extended Bookings
   ([SDK](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/sdk/backend-modules/bookings/extended-bookings/query-extended-bookings) | [REST](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/bookings/bookings/bookings-reader-v2/query-extended-bookings)).
   Make sure to specify `bookedEntity.item.schedule.serviceId` as a filter.
1. Sum up the values for `attendance.numberOfAttendees` from all returned extended
   bookings. This lets you know how many people have already booked a place in
   the course.
1. Subtract the number of booked participants from the service's
   `defaultCapacity` to calculate the remaining spots.
1. Let the customer choose how many participants they want to book. Make
   sure the intended `numberOfParticipants` doesn't exceed the number of
   remaining places.
1. Call Create Booking
   ([SDK](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/sdk/backend-modules/bookings/bookings/create-booking) | [REST](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/bookings/bookings/bookings-writer-v2/create-booking))
   and Specify the relevant schedule ID as `booking.bookedEntity.scheduleId`. If
   the course's availability hasn't changed since calling Query Extended Bookings,
   the call succeeds and the status of the booking is `CREATED`.

Then, you could handle the payment process with Wix eCommerce
([SDK](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/sdk/backend-modules/bookings/bookings/sample-flows#backend-modules_bookings_bookings_part-2-handle-the-payment-process-with-wix-ecommerce) | [REST](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/bookings/sample-booking-flows#part-2-handle-the-payment-process-with-wix-ecommerce))
or with a custom checkout
([SDK](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/sdk/backend-modules/bookings/bookings/sample-flows#backend-modules_bookings_bookings_part-2-handle-the-payment-process-with-a-custom-checkout) | [REST](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/bookings/sample-booking-flows#part-2-handle-the-payment-process-with-a-custom-checkout)).

<Note>

Calling Create Booking
([SDK](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/sdk/backend-modules/bookings/bookings/create-booking) | [REST](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/bookings/bookings/bookings-writer-v2/create-booking))
can fail if the service is no longer available. You
can ensure success, for example if you want to create a booking on behalf
of the business owner. Then, use Create Booking with
`options.flowControlSettings.skipAvailabilityValidation` set to `true`. Using other
`flowControlSettings` lets you override the maximum number of participants per
session, or allow payment methods that aren't supported for the service.

</Note>
