## Build a customer onboarding and point management system

Create a system that automatically enrolls new customers, manages their point transactions, and provides personalized account dashboards.

1. When a new customer registers on a site, call [Create Account](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/api-reference/crm/loyalty-program/accounts/accounts/create-account) with their `contactId` to establish their loyalty account. Save the returned `accountId`.
1. Call [Earn Points](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/api-reference/crm/loyalty-program/accounts/accounts/earn-points) with the `accountId` field to give the customer welcome bonus points (for example, 500 points for signing up).
1. Call [Get Loyalty Program](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/api-reference/crm/loyalty-program/loyalty-program-management/program/get-loyalty-program) to retrieve the program configuration and display earning opportunities to the new customer.
1. Call [List Earning Rules](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/api-reference/crm/loyalty-program/loyalty-program-management/earning-rules/list-earning-rules) to show customers all the ways they can earn points in your program.
1. When customers view their account dashboard, call [Get Current Member Account](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/api-reference/crm/loyalty-program/accounts/accounts/get-current-member-account) to display their current point balance, tier status, and account details.
