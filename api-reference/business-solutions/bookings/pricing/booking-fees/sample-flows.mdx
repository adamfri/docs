This article shares some possible use cases you could support,
as well as a sample flow that could support each use case.
This can be a helpful jumping off point as you plan your implementation.

## Collect a cancellation fee

You could collect a cancellation fee from the customer when the business
cancels a booking in their dashboard on behalf of the customer. Note that Wix
Bookings automatically applies the cancellation fee to an eCommerce order if
the customer cancels the booking themselves.

To collect a cancellation fee:

1. Listen to *Booking Canceled*
   ([SDK](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/sdk/backend-modules/bookings/bookings/on-booking-canceled) | [REST](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/bookings/bookings/bookings-writer-v2/booking-canceled))
   and save the booking ID.
1. Confirm that the business has canceled the booking in the dashboard by checking
   that `initiatedBy` is set to a `wixUserId`. Don't apply cancellation fees
   for bookings that were canceled by customers themselves, since Wix Bookings
   does that automatically.
1. Calculate the resulting booking fees with
   *List Booking Fees By Booking Ids*
   ([SDK](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/sdk/backend-modules/bookings/booking-fees/list-booking-fees-by-booking-ids) | [REST](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/bookings/pricing/booking-fees/list-booking-fees-by-booking-ids)).
1. Apply the booking fees to an eCommerce order, calling
   *Apply Booking Fees to Order*
   ([SDK](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/sdk/backend-modules/bookings/booking-fees/apply-booking-fees-to-order) | [REST](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/bookings/pricing/booking-fees/apply-booking-fees-to-order)).
1. Charge the customer with *Collect Applied Booking Fees*
   ([SDK](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/sdk/backend-modules/bookings/booking-fees/collect-applied-booking-fees) | [REST](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/bookings/pricing/booking-fees/collect-applied-booking-fees)).

## Create a cancellation fee dashboard

You could display a dashboard to the business owners that allows them to
manage cancellation fees for their bookings.

To create a cancellation fee dashboard:

1. Call *Query Extended Booking*
   ([SDK](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/sdk/backend-modules/bookings/extended-bookings/query-extended-bookings) | [REST](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/bookings/bookings/bookings-reader-v2/query-extended-bookings))
   and specify a time range as `query.filter`. This retrieves all
   upcoming bookings. If you want to display only bookings for a specific list
   of services, also specify the relevant service IDs in the filter. Save the
   list of retrieved booking IDs.
1. Display each booking to the business owners including a button to manage the
   related cancellation fee.
1. When the business owner clicks the manage cancellation fee button, call
   *List Booking Fees By Booking Ids*
   ([SDK](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/sdk/backend-modules/bookings/booking-fees/list-booking-fees-by-booking-ids) | [REST](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/bookings/pricing/booking-fees/list-booking-fees-by-booking-ids))
   to retrieve the associated fees. You could display them in a new modal.
1. Make sure to not display fees with a status of `PREVIEW` since the customer hasn't
   canceled the associated booking.
1. In the modal, you may include a button that allows the business to discount
   the cancellation fee and another button to apply the fee to the corresponding
   eCommerce order.
1. Make sure to save discounted fee prices on your servers until the business is
   ready to apply them.
1. Call *Apply Booking Fees to Order*
   ([SDK](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/sdk/backend-modules/bookings/booking-fees/apply-booking-fees-to-order) | [REST](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/bookings/pricing/booking-fees/apply-booking-fees-to-order))
   to apply the relevant booking fees as a single additional fee to an
   eCommerce order. To apply a discounted fee, specify a `priceOverride`
   object with the amount stored on your servers.

## Cancel a booking on behalf of the customer

You could display information about the fee customers would have to be before
they cancel their booking.

To display cancellation fee details:

1. Call *Query Extended Booking*
   ([SDK](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/sdk/backend-modules/bookings/extended-bookings/query-extended-bookings) | [REST](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/bookings/bookings/bookings-reader-v2/query-extended-bookings)),
   specifying `{"withBookingPolicySettings": true}` and a time range as `query.filter`.
   This retrieves all bookings in the given range including their booking policies.
1. Display each booking to the customer. Include a cancel button, if the
   extended booking's  `allowedActions.cancel` field is set to `true`.
1. When the customer clicks the cancel button check if the relevant
   *booking policy snapshot's*
  ([SDK](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/sdk/backend-modules/bookings/booking-policy-snapshots/introduction) | [REST](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/bookings/policies/booking-policy-snapshots/introduction))
   `cancellationFeePolicy.enabled` is set to `true`. Then, call
   *List Booking Fees By Booking Ids*
  ([SDK](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/sdk/backend-modules/bookings/booking-fees/list-booking-fees-by-booking-ids) | [REST](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/bookings/pricing/booking-fees/list-booking-fees-by-booking-ids))
   and specify the relevant `bookingId`. Note that the returned cancellation
   fee has `{"status": "PREVIEW"}`, which means that the customer hasn't agreed
   to pay it yet.
1. Display the cancellation fee to the customer. After they've agreed to pay
   the fee, call *Cancel Booking*
   ([SDK](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/sdk/backend-modules/bookings/bookings/cancel-booking) | [REST](fallback::https://dev.wix.com/docs/rest/business-solutions/bookings/bookings/bookings-writer-v2/cancel-booking)).
1. Wix Bookings automatically applies the cancellation fee to the order, once
   the booking has been canceled.
