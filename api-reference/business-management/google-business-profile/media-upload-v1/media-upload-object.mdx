title: "Media Upload Object"
description: "One queued photo change on a Google Business Profile location: either an addition or a removal."
icon: "cube"
---

One queued photo change on a Google Business Profile location: either an
addition or a removal.

Photo changes are queued and applied gradually rather than immediately,
because Google accepts only about 10 edits per minute per Business Profile.

Anything you can read here is either still waiting to be applied or has
failed. A change that succeeded leaves nothing behind, so an empty list means
the queue finished and everything worked. See `state`.

<Tabs>

  <Tab title="REST">

    <ResponseField name="id" type="string">

      Queued change ID.

      **Read-only.** Constraints: `format GUID`.

    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="locationId" type="string">

      ID of the location this change applies to. This is Google's location
      identifier.

      Constraints: `minLength 1`, `maxLength 255`, `immutable`.

    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="batchId" type="string">

      Groups the changes queued by one Bulk Create Media Uploads call. Useful for
      troubleshooting, since one save can produce more than one batch.

      **Read-only.** Constraints: `format GUID`.

    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="slotKey" type="string">

      Your own key for this photo: `logo`, `cover`, or `photo:<id>`.

      Set it to something stable and meaningful to you. It's how a failure is
      matched back to the photo that caused it, and it's part of what makes a
      repeated submission a duplicate rather than a second change.

      Constraints: `maxLength 255`.

    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="sourceUrl" type="string">

      Publicly reachable URL Google fetches the image from. Additions only.

      Keep it reachable well beyond the upload itself. A failure report renders
      this URL as a thumbnail, possibly weeks later.

      Constraints: `maxLength 2048`.

    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="category" type="string">

      Where the photo appears on the profile. Additions only.

      **Supported values:**

      - `COVER` — The banner image at the top of the profile. One per location.
      - `PROFILE` — The profile image shown alongside the business name.
      - `LOGO` — The business's logo. One per location.
      - `EXTERIOR` — The outside of the premises, including the storefront.
      - `INTERIOR` — The inside of the premises.
      - `PRODUCT` — A product the business sells.
      - `AT_WORK` — The team at work, such as staff serving customers.
      - `FOOD_AND_DRINK` — Food or drink the business serves.
      - `MENU` — The menu, for a business that serves food or drink.
      - `COMMON_AREA` — A shared space, such as a lobby or a waiting area.
      - `ROOMS` — Guest rooms, for accommodation businesses.
      - `TEAMS` — The people who work at the business.
      - `ADDITIONAL` — Anything that doesn't fit the other categories. This is the default for a
      gallery photo.

    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="format" type="string">

      Media type of the photo. Additions only.

      **Supported values:**

      - `PHOTO`
      - `VIDEO`

    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="operation" type="string">

      Whether this change adds a photo to the profile or removes one.

      **Supported values:**

      - `CREATE` — Add a photo to the profile.
      - `DELETE` — Remove a photo from the profile.

    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="sequence" type="integer">

      Position in the order the queue applies changes, assigned by the server.

      Every removal comes before every addition, because Google rejects new photos
      on a profile that's already at its limit, so the old ones have to go first.
      Additions then follow as logo, cover, and gallery in the order given.

      **Read-only.**

    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="state" type="string">

      Whether this change is waiting, being applied, or has failed.

      **Read-only.**

      **Supported values:**

      - `PENDING` — Waiting its turn in the queue.
      - `UPLOADING` — Being applied to Google now. A change that stays here isn't one that never
      ran: its outcome isn't known yet.
      - `FAILED` — Failed, and won't be retried. Kept until replaced or dismissed.

    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="attempts" type="integer">

      How many times this change has been attempted.

      **Read-only.**

    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="mediaKey" type="string">

      Google's identifier for the photo to remove. Removals only.

      Constraints: `maxLength 255`.

    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="failure" type="MediaUploadFailure">

      Why this change failed. Present only when `state` is `FAILED`.

      **Read-only.**

      <Expandable title="failure properties">

        <ResponseField name="reason" type="string">

          Why the change failed, in terms you can show a site owner.

          **Supported values:**

          - `IMAGE_TOO_SMALL` — Below Google's minimum dimensions.
          - `ASPECT_RATIO_REJECTED` — Cover/logo ratio outside Google's accepted range.
          - `FORMAT_UNSUPPORTED` — File type Google will not accept.
          - `SOURCE_UNREACHABLE` — Google could not fetch the image URL.
          - `PERMISSION_REVOKED` — Access to the Google account was lost. The site owner needs to connect
          again.
          - `RATE_LIMIT_EXHAUSTED` — Google was still rate limiting after the queue used up its retries.
          - `UPLOAD_UNVERIFIED` — The change may or may not have reached Google, and the queue chose not to
          risk a duplicate by trying again. Additions only.

          Unlike every other reason here, this doesn't say anything is wrong with the
          photo. It says the outcome couldn't be confirmed. Word it for the site owner
          that way: "we couldn't confirm this photo was added", not "this photo was
          rejected".

        </ResponseField>

      </Expandable>

    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="createdDate" type="datetime">

      Date and time the change was queued.

      **Read-only.**

    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="updatedDate" type="datetime">

      Date and time the change was last updated.

      **Read-only.**

    </ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="revision" type="string">

      Revision number, incremented on every update.

      **Read-only.**

    </ResponseField>

    ## Example

    <CodeGroup>

      ```json Object
      {
        "mediaUpload": {
          "id": "7b6a2e10-84cf-4d95-a0e3-52c9b7d16f48",
          "locationId": "locations/8117348495523092385",
          "batchId": "e0b2745c-9f31-4a8d-b6c7-1d4e58a9f302",
          "slotKey": "cover",
          "sourceUrl": "https://static.wixstatic.com/media/bakery-storefront.jpg",
          "category": "COVER",
          "format": "PHOTO",
          "operation": "CREATE",
          "sequence": 1001,
          "state": "FAILED",
          "attempts": 3,
          "failure": {
            "reason": "ASPECT_RATIO_REJECTED"
          },
          "createdDate": "2026-08-24T09:15:22.481Z",
          "updatedDate": "2026-08-24T09:18:41.905Z",
          "revision": "4"
        }
      }
      ```

    </CodeGroup>
